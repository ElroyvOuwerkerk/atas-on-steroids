# ChartChampions Vault — full manifest

Complete list of the member-downloadable Vault (`portal.chartchampions.com/vault`), captured
2026-11-12. Three sections: Daily Update Sheets, Templates (actual workspace/layout files), and
Cheat Sheets (high-res PDF summaries of the lessons).

**How downloads work:** clicking a card downloads the file to `~/Downloads` (PDF for sheets/cheat
sheets; a zip/workspace file for templates), and opens a throwaway browser tab. There is no bulk
download. **The 71 cheat-sheet PDFs are condensed visual versions of lesson material already captured
in full under `notes/`** — this manifest lets any specific one be grabbed in one click rather than
duplicating all of them. The genuinely unique items are the **ATAS workspace/template files** and the
**daily ES/NQ level sheets**; those are flagged ★ and pulled to `~/Downloads/ChartChampions-Vault/`.
