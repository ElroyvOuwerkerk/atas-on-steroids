# Module notes: Trading Psychology (Foundation)

Personal study notes distilled from the ChartChampions portal. Captured 2026-11-12. 5 lessons
(Apprentice level). Not indicator material, but it is the discipline layer behind the mechanical rules
— and it reinforces the risk rules the playbook already encodes (loss-streak sizing, no-trade is a
trade, keep losses small). Kept to the actionable principles.

---

## 1. Psychology Introduction — `Psychology-introduction` ★
The long, comprehensive lesson. Key principles:
- **Fear = a sizing signal.** If a trade feels uncomfortable or costs you sleep, the position is too
  large — scale down until execution is emotionless. Define your **"uncle point"** (max loss) before
  entering.
- **Emotions to watch:** fear (→ premature exits / revenge trading after a losing streak), greed +
  euphoria (→ oversized winners, feeling invincible). **Marty Schwartz:** reduce size or take a break
  after *euphoric* periods too, not only after losses.
- **No trade is also a trade** — standing aside in a rangeless/edgeless market is correct.
- **Pre-trade checklist:** do the TA, stack confluence (order block / fib / fan / horizontal), pick
  size that fits the account, execute without hesitation. Even a **70% setup loses 30% of the time** —
  keep risk constant.
- **Think in 100 trades**; size to survive the drawdowns. **Keep losses small, winners larger**
  (edge can come from win rate *or* from winner size). **Don't marry a bias** — flip when structure
  changes; the market doesn't care about being right. Trade around pivots (long/short/long) beats
  ego-holding one position. Separate accounts for swing vs scalp. Healthy sleep/nutrition/exercise
  measurably improve execution.

## 4. Summary of Trading Mindset — `Trading-mindset` ★
The systematic-execution framework:
- **Understand risk vs accept risk:** most traders admit they *can* lose but can't emotionally
  *accept* the loss in the position → they hold past invalidation hoping for break-even. **Exit the
  moment the trade is invalidated**, regardless of what price does after.
- **We are the casino (Mark Douglas):** unknown outcome on any single trade, but a probabilistic edge
  applied consistently nets profit over the sample. **Weighted-coin analogy** — edge only shows over a
  series.
- **The $1 challenge:** trade one defined setup **25 times at $1 each** to prove the edge and remove
  size-based psychology; only adjust entries/stops/targets from the **completed 25-trade data set**,
  never mid-sample or from emotion. If bigger size makes you tamper with stops, go back to smaller.
- **Robotic execution** from defined criteria + proven stats, not intuition/hope. **Indicator-stacking
  trap:** adding MACD/RSI/paid tools to avoid losses misunderstands markets — no indicator predicts
  large participants. Example rule set: buy a CC retrace at VWAP in an uptrend, stop at the swing low,
  target the -0.618.

## 5. Gaining Confidence — taking trades / handling losses — `trading-confidence`
(Victor & George.) **Confidence comes from preparation + proven results:** study, paper trade, then
scale size gradually. **Be strict with rules, flexible with ideas** (adapt to conditions). Expect the
unexpected — you have probabilities, not control. **Handling losses:** no revenge trading; give
yourself time to process, analyse what to improve, move on. Risk management + correct sizing are what
keep confidence and emotional control intact.
