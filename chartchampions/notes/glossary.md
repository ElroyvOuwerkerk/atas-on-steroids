# ChartChampions Glossary

Full glossary from the CC member portal (`portal.chartchampions.com/glossary`), captured
2026-11-12. Every term with its definition and, where the portal gives one, the lesson it is
taught in (module + timestamp). This is the authoritative source for CC's own abbreviations —
when a note or an indicator uses a short code (COT, COC, CCV, OTF, RF…), the exact CC meaning is
here. Private study reference, not for redistribution.

---

## A

**A/B TPO** — Above/Below Time Point of Control. Time spent above vs below the time POC.
*(Contenders TPO series part 4, 8:44)*

**A/B VOL** — Above/Below Volume Point of Control. Volume above vs below the POC.
*(Contenders TPO series part 4, 9:30)*

**Aggressive order** — A trader executing to buy/sell at market. Happens when spreads and depth
are low; removes liquidity. *(Footprint: Delta, 12:37)*

**All Time High (ATH)** — Highest value ever recorded. Targets found via Fibonacci Expansion.
*(Strategies: Trading All Time Highs, 24:05; Fibonacci: Expansion, 21:18)*

**All Time Low (ATL)** — Lowest value ever recorded. Targets via Fibonacci Expansion.
*(Fibonacci: Expansion, 20:55)*

**Automatic Rally (ARA)** — Caused by intense selling (SC); little buying pressure needed to
rally, fuelled by shorts covering. *(Champion Livestream 32, 43:15; Contenders Live Charting, 25:40)*

**Automatic Reaction (AR)** — Wyckoff distribution: a sell-off fuelled by late longs getting
rekt; OI decreases or stays level; the bottom gives the range low. *(Accumulation & Distribution II, 13:21)*

**Average Candle Range (ACR)** — The average candle size during the day. *(Contenders TPO Series
Part 4, 12:14; Advanced Strategies: CCTR, 8:27)* — used for CCTR exits.

**Average down** — Adding to a losing position on a fresh setup (advanced). Daniel often closes at
break-even after a bounce, reviews, and re-enters with a fresh mind. Always put the SL at
invalidation.

**Average True Range (ATR)** — Volatility indicator; average move over a timeframe. Higher in more
volatile markets. Daniel uses it for stocks. *(Indicators, 30:25; 50SMA Strategy Q&A, 37:45 for SL use)*

## B

**Backtesting** — Evaluating a strategy on historical data. *(Strategies: How to record statistics)*

**Backup (BU)** — Wyckoff accumulation: old AR highs are tested, resistance flips to support,
price moves higher. *(Accumulation & Distribution I, 43:44)*

**Bearish Order Block** — The last "up" close candle at/near a key resistance. Confirmed only when
the next candle trades below its low. *(Advanced Strategies: Order Blocks, 10:15)*

**Bear market** — Price continuously declining over a longer period; lower highs, selling pressure
dominant.

**Bollinger Bands (BB)** — Upper/lower band + a middle SMA; measure deviation (tighten when quiet,
widen when volatile); mark oversold/overbought. By John Bollinger. *(Strategies: CCSS, 13:25)*

**Break even (BE)** — Neither a loss nor a win. *(Rivalry AMA 9, 58:35 for the ATAS BE exit)*

**BTC** — Bitcoin. First decentralised digital cryptocurrency; peer-to-peer, permission-less.

**Bullish Order Block** — The last "down" close candle at/near a key support. Confirmed only when
the next candle trades above its high. *(Advanced Strategies: Order Blocks, 9:12)*

**Bull trap** — A false signal that a down-trend has reversed up, when price will keep declining.

**Buying Climax (BC)** — Wyckoff distribution: retail buying reaches a climax, often with very
bullish news. *(Accumulation & Distribution II, 10:51)*

**Buying Tail (BT)** — Like a single print but at the low of the TPO profile; a larger trader
stepped in to reject price. *(TPO Series: Single prints, 16:40)*

## C

**CC Fib Level** — The Champions Channel Fibonacci level: the ratios between .618 and .66.

**Champions Channel (CC)** — The Fibonacci ratios between 0.618 and 0.66. *(Fibonacci: Retracement, 40:30)*

**Change of Context (COC)** — A reversal candle that breaks the previous candle's high/low
(bullish takes prior high, bearish takes prior low), confirming the 1-minute context flipped.
*(3-Candle Entry Strategy Refinement Part I)*

**Chart Champions Value (CCV)** — Valid when the market opens outside the previous day's VAH/VAL
and then gets accepted back inside (two consecutive 30-min closes). Then ~80% chance price touches
the previous day's opposite VAL/VAH. *(Strategies: CCV Setup, 7:58)*

**Chart Champions Wizardry (CCW)** — Beginner-recommended; Fibonacci levels plus at least two
extra confluence factors. A mechanical practice style. *(Strategies: CCW Setup)*

**Chart of the week (COTW)** — A "Skill Sharpener" exercise: identify a high-probability setup
before the next livestream and post it in Discord with entry/exit/stop reasoning.

**Cherry On Top (COT)** — A reversal candle that sweeps prior liquidity (bullish sweeps prior low,
bearish sweeps prior high) and closes in the extreme quartile.
*(3-Candle Entry Strategy Refinement Part I)*

**CME** — Chicago Mercantile Exchange; organized exchange for futures and options.

**Commitment of Traders (COT)** — A weekly sentiment report on the positioning of different trading
pairs. *(note: same abbreviation as Cherry On Top — context disambiguates)*

**Completed 13** — The final countdown signal, printed only when all countdown completion rules are
met. *(Sequence by Igor)*

**Compounding** — Already in a setup when another setup presents within it; taking it compounds the
position. You'd take the trade regardless — it is a trade itself. *(Entering Trades: Compounding, 24:58)*

**Consolidation** — Price moving sideways; can break either way, continuation of trend favoured.
Typically follows an impulsive move. *(Entering Trades: Consolidation)*

**Crypto Twitter (CT)** — The part of Twitter talking crypto/trading.

**Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD)** — Running total of delta; buys on the ask added, sells on the bid
subtracted. Shows buying/selling pressure. *(Footprint: CVD, 13:35)*

## D

**Day Trading** — Opening and closing positions within the same day; avoids overnight risk.
*(Type of Traders Part I)*

**Dead Cat Bounce (DCB)** — Small, temporary recovery in a declining asset; the most common bear-
market retracement.

**Decentralized Exchange (DEX)** — Crypto exchange without a central authority; peer-to-peer.

**Delta** — Sum of aggressive trades; shows who was in control during the candle (buys minus
sells). Positive or negative; confirms breakouts instantly. *(Footprints: Delta, 12:35)*

**Depth of Market (DOM)** — Liquidity of a market; the Order Book — price and the limit buy/sell
orders at each price. *(Advanced Strategies: Level 2 - Time & Sales)*

**Dollar Cost Average (DCA)** — Placing a fixed dollar amount into an asset on a regular schedule.

**Dom Surface (DSf)** — A tool showing the supply/demand ratio difference in real time and the
formation of strong S/R levels. *(Dom Surface Liquidity Heatmap)* — this is the DSF the Severin
notes reference; it is a Quantower heatmap tool.

**Double Distribution (DD)** — Price gets acceptance into a larger row of single prints between two
distribution profiles. Cannot occur on the same day, but can the next day up to a week later.
*(TPO Series: Ledges & DD Setups, 16:00)*

**Downtrend** — A series of lower highs and lower lows. *(Basic: Market Structure Part I, 9:15)*

**DYOR** — Do Your Own Research; don't blindly follow others.

**dPOC / dVAH / dVAL** — Daily POC / value area high / value area low. See POC / VAH / VAL.

## E

**EAP** — Exactly As Predicted; used when a trading plan results in a complete win.

**Electronic Trading Hours (ETH)** — The main full session of an asset per its spec; includes pre-
and post-market data.

**Elliott Wave (EW)** — Theory based on the fractal nature of markets; for predicting prices and
targets. By Ralph Nelson Elliott. *(Elliott Wave module)*

**Equilibrium (EQ)** — The state where supply and demand balance. (Intraday: the midpoint/halfback
of a range — a reliable pullback entry.)

**ES** — E-Mini S&P-500 futures (CME).

**Exchange-Traded Funds (ETF)** — A diversified portfolio of securities bundled in one fund.
*(Long Term Stock Market Strategy Part I)*

**Exponential Moving Average (EMA)** — Trend-direction MA weighting recent data more; a lagging
indicator. *(Strategies: EMA Strategy, 18:15)*

## F

**Failed Auction** — Price attempts to break a range high/low, then reverses and is accepted back
inside. Marks weakness/strength; clean entry and invalidation, high R:R. *(Advanced Strategies: Failed Auction)*

**Fear of Missing Out (FOMO)** — Entering trades without thought for fear of missing profit; a
discipline failure. Always have a plan with entry, invalidation and TP.

**Final Downside Target (FDT)** — ORB strategy: 1.5× the opening range below its low; a downside
objective on a bearish break with bearish VWAP alignment. *(Opening Range Breakout Strategy)*

**Final Upside Target (FUT)** — ORB strategy: 1.5× the opening range above its high; an upside
objective on a bullish break with bullish VWAP alignment. *(Opening Range Breakout Strategy)*

**Footprint Bar Statistics (FPBS)** — An ExoCharts feature aggregating Volume, Delta, CVD, POC,
candle highs/lows, build time, trade count. *(Footprint: Exo Charts, 37:14)*

**Fractal** — Price action similar to a previous movement. *(Contenders Fractal, 5:13)*

**Frontrun** — Trading a level that is slightly missed/not hit — the level was "frontrun".
*(Advanced Strategies: Frontrun)*

**FUD** — Fear, Uncertainty, Doubt; believed to drop a targeted asset's price.

**Fundamental Analysis (FA)** — Assessing intrinsic value via qualitative and quantitative factors.

## G

**Golden Pocket (GP)** — The Fibonacci ratios between .618 and .65. See Champions Channel.

**Good till Cancelled (GTC)** — An order that stays active until filled or cancelled.

## H

**Head and Shoulders (H&S)** — A top reversal pattern: three peaks, the middle highest.
*(Patterns: Head and Shoulders, 17:25)*

**Higher High (HH)** — A high higher than the previous one. *(Basic: Market Structure)*

**Higher Low (HL)** — A low higher than the previous one. *(Basic: Market Structure)*

**High Time Frame (HTF)** — Typically a 4-hour+ timeframe.

**High Volume Node (HVN)** — A volume-profile area where a large amount of volume traded.
*(Volume Part 3 - VPVR, 20:43; Rivalry AMA 23, 50:40)*

**HODL** — Hold On for Dear Life; buy/accumulate with no intention to sell soon.

## I

**Iceberg Order** — Used to fill very big positions; only part is visible, it keeps refilling.
*(Contenders Order Book, 8:41)*

**iH&S** — Inverse Head and Shoulders; a bottoming pattern after a big sell-off. *(10.7.19 stream, 31:10)*

**Initial Balance (IB)** — The first 1 hour of the trading day. *(Strategies: Daily Opens, 18:26)*

**Initial Balance High / Low (IBH / IBL)** — The high / low of the Initial Balance.

**ITM** — In-The-Money option: a call (put) whose strike is below (above) current price.

## L

**Laddering** — Placing multiple buy/sell orders to get an average entry price.
*(Compounding, averaging down and laddering stream, 39:05)*

**Last Point of Supply (LPS)** — The last point of a major R/S flip. *(Accumulation & Distribution II, 29:23)*

**Ledges** — Poor highs/lows formed inside the TPO chart: two or more TPO blocks at the same price.
*(TPO Series: Ledges & DD Setup, 8:05)*

**Liquidity** — The ease of converting an asset to cash without affecting price. Essential.
*(Entering trades: SFP and Liquidity, 4:20)*

**London Stock Exchange (LSE)** — Here used for the London session open: 07:00 UTC / 08:00 local.

**Lower High (LH)** — A high lower than the previous one. *(Basic: Market Structure)*

**Lower Low (LL)** — A low lower than the previous one. *(Basic: Market Structure)*

**Low Time Frame (LTF)** — A small period, ~1 minute up to 1 hour.

**Low Volume Cluster / Node (LVC / LVN)** — An area where little volume traded.
*(Volume Part 3 - VPVR, 20:43; Rivalry AMA 23, 50:40)*

## M

**Market Maker (MM)** — Quotes both buy and sell prices to provide liquidity.

**Market Profile (MP)** — Charting technique showing price, volume and time on one chart; aka TPO.
*(TPO Part I; Rivalry's Profile Trading Part I, 2:17)*

**Market Structure (MS)** — The S/R levels, swing highs and lows; a trend-following tool; bullish
or bearish. *(Basic: Market Structure)*

**Mid Timeframe (MTF)** — Typically 1 hour to 4 hours.

**Momentum Entry** — A clean supply/demand break with an extreme-quartile close, entry on the next
candle's retest, tight local stop (retest candle). *(3-Candle Entry Strategy Refinement Part II)*

**MACD** — Trend-following momentum indicator; 12 EMA minus 26 EMA.

**Moving Average (MA)** — A lagging indicator based on past prices; shows trend and acts as S/R.

**mPOC / mVAH / mVAL** — Monthly POC / VAH / VAL. See POC / VAH / VAL.

## N

**Naked POC (nPOC)** — A POC from the previous session not yet tapped. Generally bounces on first
approach; look for confluence. *(Volume Part 5: Session Volume + Naked POCs, 35:41)*

**New York VWAP (NY VWAP)** — The VWAP line from the New York session; effective on ES where most
volume transacts.

**Notional Value** — Total value of a futures contract: contract size × market price.
*(ATAS & ES Know-How)*

**NQ** — E-mini Nasdaq-100 futures (CME).

## O

**On Balance Volume (OBV)** — Cumulative total of positive/negative volume; a leading indicator
(volume leads price); spots divergences. *(Volume Part 2 - OBV, 34:25)*

**One Time Framing (OTF)** — Price moving up/down without breaking a high/low in the opposite
direction = a strong trend; shows which side is in control. *(Rivalry AMA 47, 57:57)*

**Open Interest (OI)** — Total number of open contracts; measures money flow into futures/options/
crypto derivatives. *(Indicators: Open Interest + Funding, 18:10)*

**Order Block (OB)** — A supply/demand view: the initial opposite move before the trend resumes to
new highs/lows. *(Advanced Strategies: Order Blocks)*

**Order Flow (OF)** — Data of contracts bought/sold at a price that moves price.
*(Footprints module; Advanced Strategies: Severin Scalping Strategy I/II/III)*

**OTM** — Out-of-The-Money option; the most aggressive options method.

**Overnight High / Low (ONH / ONL)** — The high / low created overnight before the New York
session; a day-timeframe reference.

## P

**Passive Order** — Adds liquidity; limit bid/ask orders, not immediately executed.
*(Footprint: Delta, 12:37)*

**pdEQ** — Previous day Equilibrium; the 0.5 fib retracement from the previous day's swing high to
low.

**pdPOC / pdVAH / pdVAL** — Previous day POC / VAH / VAL.

**Perfected Setup** — A higher-quality TD setup confirmed when the indicator prints an arrow after
a structural extreme. Perfected Buy: low of bar 8 or 9 ≤ lows of bars 6 and 7. Perfected Sell:
high of bar 8 or 9 ≥ highs of bars 6 and 7. *(Sequence by Igor)*

**Periodic Volume Profile (PVP)** — A sequence of volume profiles over specific periods.

**pmPOC / pmVAH / pmVAL** — Previous month POC / VAH / VAL.

**Point of Control (POC)** — The price with the largest traded volume over a period; a big
horizontal S/R line. *(Volume Part 3 - VPVR, 22:36)*

**Poor High** — A market profile with no selling tail; expect the high to be taken out (move up or
an SFP that builds a selling tail). *(TPO Part 5 - Single Prints, 17:30)*

**Poor Low** — A market profile with no buying tail; expect the low to be taken out (move down or
an SFP that builds a buying tail). *(TPO Part 5 - Single Prints, 17:30)*

**Position Trader** — Focuses on investments; weekly+ timeframe, years ahead. *(Basic: Types of Traders Part 1)*

**Power Hour** — The last hour of the New York session; good volatility for scalps.

**Preliminary Supply (PSY)** — Wyckoff: after an extended uptrend, smart money takes profit on
longs and builds shorts. *(Accumulation & Distribution II, 9:22)*

**Preliminary Support (PS)** — Wyckoff: after a big sell-off, substantial buying provides support;
starts accumulation (first bounce of the range). *(Accumulation & Distribution I, 22:10)*

**Price Action (PA)** — Reading basic price movement across a timeframe; subjective decisions on
recent movement. See Market Structure. *(Basic: Market Structure)*

**Pulling** — Removing orders from the order book. *(Order Book, 16:13)*

**pwPOC / pwVAH / pwVAL** — Previous week POC / VAH / VAL.

## R

**Regular Trading Hours (RTH)** — A reduced session, 09:30–16:30 (UTC-4); basically the New York
session.

**Relative Strength Index (RSI)** — A lagging indicator measuring strength/weakness from recent
closes. *(Indicators, 59:48)*

**Rotation Factor (RF)** — Each 30-minute column scored +1 or -1; the running total shows which way
the market attempted to push. *(TPO Part 4: Template, 33:00)*

**R:R** — Risk:Reward. Should be at least 2:1, preferably 3:1.

## S

**Satoshi (Sat)** — Smallest sub-unit of Bitcoin: 1 Sat = 1/100,000,000 BTC.

**Scalp Trading** — Profiting off small price changes in a short period; needs discipline and TA.
*(Basic: Types of Traders)*

**Secondary Test (ST)** — Wyckoff accumulation: price revisits the SC area, tests demand,
resistance flips to support, then a new high (often pushed down again to SFP the lows).
*(Accumulation & Distribution I, 32:10)* — In distribution: revisits BC, support flips to
resistance, then a new low. *(Accumulation & Distribution II, 14:51)*

**SEC** — Securities and Exchange Commission.

**Selling Climax (SC)** — Heavy (retail) selling absorbed by professional interest; little volume
needed to push price back up (AR). *(Accumulation & Distribution I, 26:05)*

**Settlement Price (Settle)** — The official daily closing price of futures/options on futures, per
the CME. *(Live Trading Session, 57:02)*

**Sign of Strength (SOS)** — Price up with increasing volume (Wyckoff); can be a market-structure
change or an S/R flip. *(Accumulation & Distribution I, 42:38; Basic: Market Structure)*

**Sign of Weakness (SOW)** — Selling off on very high volume toward support (Wyckoff); sets up the
final rally before distribution concludes. *(Accumulation & Distribution II, 16:39)*

**Simple Moving Average (SMA)** — Average price over a period with equal weighting.
*(Strategies: 50 SMA Strategy)*

**Single Print (SP)** — Like a wick but in the middle of the price action. *(TPO Part 5 Single
Print, 8:15)*

**Slippage** — A quick drop with low liquidity; e.g. SL set at 5% but filled at 25%.
*(Volume Part I, 26:10)*

**Smart Tape** — An advanced order-flow tool organizing trade data to spot large orders, hidden
liquidity and moves. *(Level 2 - Time & Sales)*

**Sniper Entry** — Knowing your level, then checking the order book for stacking bids / removed
asks as price arrives. Fast; react quickly. *(Order Book Live Stream, 18:32)*

**Spoofed Order** — A fake order with no intent to fill; disappears when price arrives.
*(Order Book Live Stream, 6:08)*

**Spot** — The price to buy and own an asset immediately (vs futures/derivatives).

**Spread** — The difference between buy and sell. Avoid big spreads (low liquidity = lose money).
*(Volume Part I, 23:18)*

**Stacking** — Adding limit orders to the order book. *(Order Book Live Stream, 16:44)*

**Stop Hunt** — Forcing traders out by pushing price to where many set stops.

**Stop Loss (SL)** — Closes a losing position at a set price; a must. Hard SL (in the order book,
mandatory for beginners/intermediate) vs soft/mental SL (pros only, needs discipline).
*(Entering Trades: Moving Stop Loss)*

**Support and Resistance (S/R)** — Support = enough demand to bounce; resistance = enough supply to
block. The more they're tested, the weaker they get. *(Basic: Support and Resistance)*

**Swing Failure Pattern (SFP)** — Price takes out a high and closes back below it, or takes out a
low and closes back above it (bullish or bearish). *(Entering Trades: SFP and Liquidity)*

**Swing Trading** — Holding longer than a day/weeks; capture larger gains within a trend.

## T

**Take Profit (TP)** — An order to (partially) close a position at a profit level; can have
multiple. *(Entering trades: How much percentage profit to take)*

**Technical Analysis (TA)** — Analysis based solely on the chart with technical tools/indicators.

**TF** — Time frame.

**The Opening Range Breakout (ORB) Strategy** — Marks the high/low of the first 15 minutes and
looks for breakouts confirmed by VWAP. *(Opening Range Breakout + VWAP Strategy)*

**Three Candle Entry Strategy (3C)** — An enhancement to the Severin scalping strategy combining
1-minute order flow with candle structure for a yes/no entry decision (almost automated).
*(Severin Scalping Strategy: Addition, 10:32)*

**Tick** — The smallest price movement of a futures contract (per-contract, set by the exchange).
*(ATAS & ES Know-How, 5:20)*

**Tick Value** — The dollar value per tick, per product. CME E-Mini S&P500 = $12.50; Micro E-mini
S&P500 = $1.25.

**Time Price Opportunity (TPO)** — A unit of time during which a price traded; used in market
profile. *(TPO Part I Introduction)*

**Trading Range** — Price consistently between a high and low for a while; top = resistance,
bottom = support. *(Basic: Market Structure Part I, 13:01)*

**TradingView (TV)** — A social charting platform for traders/investors. *(Beginners: TradingView
Introduction)*

**Trend** — The general direction / momentum of a price. *(Basic: How to Spot a Trend)*

**TVAH / TVAL** — Time value area high / low. See TPO and VAH / VAL.

## U

**Upthrust After Distribution (UTAD)** — Wyckoff distribution: price goes above resistance and
prior peaks with conviction, can hold for days/weeks, then breaks down back to support and into a
confirmed downtrend. Occurs only occasionally. *(Accumulation & Distribution II, 21:37)*

**Upthrust (UT)** — Wyckoff distribution: a rally from range low to range high, ending in an SFP /
failed auction of the range high above the BC. *(Accumulation & Distribution II, 19:00)*

**Uptrend** — A series of higher highs and higher lows. *(Basic: Market Structure Part I, 3:32)*

## V

**Value Area High (VAH)** — The highest price in the marked value area; resistance while ranging.
*(Volume Part 3 – VPVR, 20:30)*

**Value Area Low (VAL)** — The lowest price in the marked value area; support while ranging.
*(Volume Part 3 – VPVR, 20:30)*

**Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP)** — Incorporates price and volume; an S/R line. Above VWAP =
"above value", below = "below value". *(Indicators: VWAP, 8:01)*

## W

**wPOC / wVAH / wVAL** — Weekly POC / VAH / VAL.

## X

**XBT** — Bitcoin ticker on some exchanges.
