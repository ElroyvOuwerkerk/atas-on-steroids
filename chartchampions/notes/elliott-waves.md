## Structure (Intro, Lessons 1-2) — `elliott-waves-introduction-part1` / `-part2`
- **Impulse = 5 waves** (1-3-5 impulse, 2-4 corrective) in the trend direction; **correction = 3
  waves (ABC)** after a completed impulse. **Fractal** — every wave contains sub-waves. Best on **4H+**
  (CC's preference). Always keep **both a bullish and a bearish count**, trade only the preferred one,
  switch to the alternate when the preferred is invalidated. EW predicts price *and* (with fib time)
  the timing.

## Impulse wave rules (Lesson 3) — `elliott-waves-impulse-waves` ★
Start on the monthly, work down; **log scale** for 1yr+ / high-volatility charts. **Adjacent-wave
rule:** adjacent waves should be the same price/time degree; the smaller shouldn't be < 1/3 of the
larger.
- **The five hard rules:** (1) **Wave 2 never retraces all of wave 1**; (2) **wave 3 longer than wave
  2**; (3) **wave 4 never retraces all of wave 3**; (4) **wave 5 > 38.2% of wave 4**; (5) **wave 3 is
  never the shortest** of 1/3/5.
- A wave-5 **failure** = wave 5 shorter than wave 3 but still above the 38.2% retrace. If impulse
  rules are broken, you're looking at corrective price action.

## Corrective wave structures (Lesson 4) — `elliott-waves-corrective-waves` ★
Three corrective types: **ZigZag (5-3-5), Flat (3-3-5), Triangle (3-3-3-3-3)**. Corrections are waves
2 and 4 (opposite the trend).
- **Wave 2 → usually a ZigZag to the CC zone; Wave 4 → usually a longer sideways Flat or Triangle.**
- **ZigZag:** wave B **< 66%** of wave A; wave C finishes **beyond the start of wave A**. (If B passes
  the 66% CC, it's not a zigzag.)
- **Flat:** wave B retraces **≥ 66%** of A. Variants — Standard (A=B=C), Failure (C < B < A), Expanded
  (B > A, C ends beyond A), Power (B < A, C ends beyond A); plus powerful-B variants. Tip: draw
  horizontal lines from A's start and end to gauge B/C strength.
- **Triangle:** traded only as sideways (ascending/descending/symmetrical; standard ABCDE symmetrical
  most common). Draw lines B→D and A→C; wave A's start and wave E's end shouldn't touch the lines.
- **Alternation** between waves 2 and 4 is expected (they differ). Trading wave 2 vs wave B often
  doesn't matter — both give the opposite-direction move once done.

## Fibonacci relationships (Lesson 6) — `elliott-waves-fibonacci-relationships` ★ targets
- **Two unextended waves relate by 61.8% or 100%** (price and time). The **extended wave** = the
  longest of 1/3/5, **≥ 161.8%** of the next longest.
- **Wave 3 ≥ 1.618× wave 1** (if extended, > 161.8%). **Wave 5 ≈ 1:1 of wave 1** (and takes ~61.8% of
  wave 1's time); if wave 5 is the extension → ~161.8% of the move from wave 1 start to wave 3 end.
  When wave 5 extends, wave 4 often retraces 50-66% of wave 3.
- **Retracements:** **wave 2 ≈ 66% (CC zone) of wave 1; wave 4 ≈ 38.2-50% of wave 3.**
- **Corrections:** Flat wave C ≈ A (1:1 / 1.618 / 0.618; after a strong B look for 1.382-1.618
  expansion); ZigZag wave C ≈ 100% of A from B's end (max 2.618× A); Triangle waves A/C/E relate by
  **66% (CC)**.

## 10. Elliott Waves "My Way" (the practical CC approach) — `elliott-waves-my-way` ★
- EW is used as a **higher-timeframe target tool, not a primary entry tool.** Daily/weekly, **no LTF
  sub-wave validation required** if the HTF count is valid. Targets via fib extensions/expansions +
  negative fibs (for ATH) and extensions + horizontals + liquidity (internal waves). **Confluence
  always required.**
- **Advanced:** willing to trade setups that break the classic rules (e.g. a triangle in wave 2, a
  3-3-3 flat) and **record statistics** — needs a **large dataset (1000+)** and a **high win rate
  (75%+)** to validate a new pattern. Beginners/intermediates should follow the rules first. Goal is
  profit, not rule-adherence.
