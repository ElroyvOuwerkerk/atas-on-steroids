# Investing and Diversifying: Part 5

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session reviews a multi-stock watchlist covering established mega caps, growth names, and community-requested tickers, with emphasis on identifying high-probability investment zones using anchored VWAPs, points of control, monthly supply/demand levels, and value-based scoring. We learn how to structure a "buy the dip" portfolio versus a long-term portfolio, how to size positions based on asset category, and how to combine technical levels with fundamental valuation to time entries during pullbacks.
