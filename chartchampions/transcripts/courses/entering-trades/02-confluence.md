# Confluence

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session presents a multi-tool technical analysis case for a potential Bitcoin bottom, combining chart patterns, Fibonacci tools, Wyckoff accumulation, volume profile, and timing techniques. We review how confluence between pattern recognition, Fibonacci retracements and extensions, Gann and pitchfork tools, and the Point of Control can strengthen the probability of a bullish reversal scenario and help identify potential targets and reaction zones.

### Highlights

- [00:00] Framing the analysis: multiple tools are combined to build a case for a potential bottom, with the acknowledgment that the setup supports at least a lower high rather than guaranteeing a continuation to new highs.
- [01:00] Fractal comparison: Early 2019 and early 2020 both show structurally similar setups Ñ a descending triangle, followed by an inverse head and shoulders, leading into a bump and run pattern. The daily close above the trendline confirms the bump and run, with a measured target near $14,000.
- [02:30] Fibonacci CC confluence: High-to-low and low-to-high retracements both react at the CC (0.618) zone. Entry at $6,888 was supported by the 618 level, weekly structure, and a swing failure pattern Ñ a strong example of confluence-based entry.
- [03:50] Monthly support concept: The 2018 monthly support that was lost and flipped to resistance acted as support again on retest. Key principle: a support level that is retested without acting as resistance often remains support.
- [05:00] Wyckoff accumulation schematic: Review of Preliminary Support, Selling Climax, Automatic Rally, Secondary Tests, Last Point of Support, Sign of Strength, and backup action. Breaking out, retesting, and holding the range strongly supports an accumulation (not distribution) interpretation.
- [05:50] Long-term trendline bounce: A multi-year trendline has repeatedly supported price, adding weight to the bullish structure.
- [06:00] Bearish Gartley harmonic pattern: A 618-to-618 structure fits the Gartley template, with a potential move toward the ~$17K region before a reversal, if the pattern completes.
- [06:50] Fibonacci channel (log scale): Drawn across major pivots (2013 low, 2015 low, cycle high), Fibonacci levels precisely captured reaction points on both the way up and the way down Ñ reinforcing the validity of Fibonacci confluence in long-term structure.
- [09:40] Three Drives pattern: Formed via sequential high-to-low CC retracements, with the 1.272Ð1.618 extensions acting as reversal zones. A liquidity run at $7,400 preceded a sharp move, with 11,000 sats as a target reference.
- [11:00] Fibonacci Speed Fan: Drawn from the 2017Ð18 high to the 2018 low. Rejections at the 0.75, 0.618, 0.5, 0.382, and 0.25 levels (the final 0.25 is also a Gann number) capped price. On the downside, the 0.5 showed a clean support-to-resistance flip.
- [13:00] Gann Fan: Anchored from the lowest 2011 monthly close to the 2013 monthly high. The fan timed the 2017Ð18 top and ultimately provided support near recent lows, aligning with a ~38% price increase from that touch.
- [14:00] Pitchfork with 1.618 extension: Supported the 2019 bottoming structure and is now being reclaimed, reinforcing the trend-support thesis.
- [14:40] Fibonacci Time Zones: Drawn from the 2011 low to the 2013 high. Notable: Multiple additional Fibonacci time grids (1-to-1 structures and a BitMEX-anchored projection) added further time-based confluence at the same zone.
  - The 2.0 extension timed the 2017Ð18 high nearly to the day.
  - The 2.618 timed the pre-rally lows.
  - The 3.0 potentially times the current low.
- [19:40] Point of Control (2017 to present): The highest-volume node acted as a precise support at the recent lows. Large participants seek value regions with historical volume for order execution, making the POC a critical structural support.
- [20:50] Trading principle: Avoid trying to time exact tops. Waiting for confirmation (e.g., shorting the next rejection) often sacrifices a small percentage of profit in exchange for significantly higher trade security.

We encourage reviewing each of these tools individually, understanding how confluence strengthens a thesis, and applying them with disciplined risk management rather than relying on any single indicator in isolation.
