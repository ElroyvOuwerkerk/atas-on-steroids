# How Much Percentage Profit To Take

## Portal Summary & Highlights

We also have cheat sheets available for this topic which can be downloaded as a high-res PDF via our vault section here : https://portal.chartchampions.com/vault - download is titled ‘Trading Setup’

Welcome to the How Much % Profit to Take lesson where coach Daniel gives an overview of how much percentage profit to take at different levels while swing trading. Stay informed, trade well!

TIMESTAMPS

00:01 – INTRO

00:38 – UTILITY

Many people take too much % profit, or too little.

Having a take profit % strategy can be very helpful/

Make sure your plan, which includes entry and SL, also includes the TP locations and percentages ahead of time.

02:10 – IMPLEMENTATION

TP % needs to be included when planning the whole trade.

Set hard TP1, TP2 and then a trailing Stop Loss.

TP1 is more of a ‘protection to minimize loss’ if SL gets hit.

There is no right or wrong % of taking profits. It is not wrong to take 90% at TP1 if that is your plan.

It depends on the context of the trade, but sometimes 50% TP1 and other times less, like 5% TP1.

TP1 can be from an intraday level, such as the VWAP or a mini range POC.

You can then take TP2 at a swing trader's high probability level, such as an overall range high or HTF strong confluence level.

?? 05:20 How Coach Daniel approached building a swing trading plan.

21:38 – TIPS & TRICKS

? Plan, plan, plan. If you do not plan your TP %, you will make big mistakes!

? You will fail if you do not take enough profits, once you change that bad habit your trading will improve massively.

? Never be annoyed about TP and then when price continues think, ‘I could have made more if I did not TP’. PROFIT IS PROFIT.
