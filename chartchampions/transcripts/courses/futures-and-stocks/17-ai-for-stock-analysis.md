# AI for Stock Analysis

## Portal Summary & Highlights

Find all files and cheat sheets in our Vault under “Master Instruction Block” here: https://portal.chartchampions.com/vault

In this comprehensive lesson, we explore how to leverage artificial intelligence for systematic stock analysis while maintaining investment discipline. The video introduces a Structured Equity Analysis Model—a nine-step framework that transforms AI-generated research into consistent, comparable investment decisions. We'll learn how to implement a fixed scoring rubric, conviction bands, and invalidation triggers that protect capital and remove emotional bias from our investment process.
