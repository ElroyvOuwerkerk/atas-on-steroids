# ES Series: TradingView Trade Execution (Contenders)

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session introduces trade execution using the TradingView platform, highlighting how it can serve as an alternative for traders who use ExoCharts for technical analysis but need a separate execution tool. We review the structure of the TradingView trading panel, available order types, how to manage take profit and stop loss levels, and practical tips for consistent trade management.

### Highlights

- [00:00] Introduction to the ES-series on TradingView trade execution, including the lesson structure: utility, implementation, and practical tips.
- [00:38] Importance of understanding your trading platform for effective trade management and seamless execution.
- [00:51] Context for using TradingView: ExoCharts users lack built-in trading functionality and can use TradingView as an execution tool.
- [01:08] Advantages of trading via TradingView:
  - Simplified workflow combining analysis and execution in one platform
  - Real-time market data integration for futures
  - Multiple brokerage integrations (e.g., AMP Futures, Forex.com, Interactive Brokers)
  - Support for advanced order types: market, limit, stop, and conditional orders
- [02:16] Overview of the trading panel: it can be used docked on the right side of the chart or undocked for a floating view.
- [02:52] Dissection of panel components Ñ buy/sell orders and selection between market, limit, and stop orders.
- [03:17] Setting the contract quantity manually or via the built-in quantity tool, and pre-defining take profit and stop loss before execution.
- [03:54] Configuring a take profit:
  - Define by number of ticks, or
  - Define by target price value
  - TradingView automatically calculates expected reward in both dollar value and percentage
- [04:20] Configuring a stop loss using ticks or price value, with automatic calculation of risk in dollar and percentage terms.
- [04:48] Key limitation: TradingView does not support take profit strategies (scaled exits). Each take profit and stop loss order must be set individually.
- [05:00] Manual trade management via drag and drop adjustment of stop loss and take profit lines directly on the chart after a position is open.
- [05:48] Reviewing executed trades through the Orders tab: shows order type, contract size, entry price, stop loss, take profit, exit price, time, and order ID Ñ all useful for journaling.
- [06:29] Using the Account History tab to review balance before and after sessions and calculate daily P&L.
- [06:50] Reference to understanding tick calculations, futures contract specifications, and fee structure for proper trade sizing and expectancy evaluation.
- [07:08] Tips and best practices:
  - Take time to familiarize yourself with your trading platform
  - Practice using the paper trading feature before live execution
  - Always double-check quantity, trading pair, and protection levels prior to execution
  - Favor pre-defined take profit and stop loss placement over drag-and-drop for consistency
  - Maintain a dedicated trading journal rather than relying solely on the account summary

We encourage reviewing these execution concepts carefully and applying them with disciplined risk management, consistent order placement, and thorough journaling.
