# Profile Trading: Part 2

## Portal Summary & Highlights

In this comprehensive session, we dive deep into Market Profile Trading Strategy - Part 2, focusing on the utility, implementation, and practical application of Time Price Opportunity (TPO) analysis. We explore how time acts as the market's regulator and learn to identify who's in control of the market by understanding initial balance, session letters (A through M), and day types. This lesson provides essential knowledge for determining whether to trade with or against the trend, a critical skill for consistent profitability in futures trading.
