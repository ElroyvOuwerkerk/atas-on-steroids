This video provides an in-depth analysis of the three drives pattern and the valleys and peaks pattern in trading. The three drives pattern consists of three attempts at a level before a reversal, with each drive extending between the 1.272 to 1.618 Fibonacci levels. The valleys and peaks pattern involves three attempts to break a high or low, often with lower volume and divergences on each attempt. The video emphasizes the importance of context and confluence when trading these patterns, rather than blindly following a cheat sheet.
