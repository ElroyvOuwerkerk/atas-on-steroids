# Entry Triggers

## Portal Summary & Highlights

We also have cheat sheets available for this topic which can be downloaded as a high-res PDF via our vault section here : https://portal.chartchampions.com/vault - download is titled ‘Entry Triggers’

This video provides a comprehensive overview of entry triggers, which are specific patterns or setups used to enter a trade after identifying a favorable trading opportunity on a higher timeframe. The key entry triggers discussed include swing failure patterns, failed auctions, mini range setups, retests, and market structure changes. The video emphasizes the importance of waiting for a clear entry trigger before taking a trade and provides guidance on how to identify and implement each trigger type.

### Timestamps:

- [00:00] Introduction and legal disclaimer
- [00:34] Explanation of entry triggers and their significance in trading
  - Entry triggers provide a precise entry reason and invalidation stop loss placement
  - Types of entry triggers: swing failure patterns, failed auctions, mini range setups, retests, and market structure changes
- [01:27] Steps to follow when an entry trigger occurs at a predetermined zone
  - Go to the 1 or 5-minute timeframe
  - Check order flow (volume, delta, open interest)
  - Wait for a shift in market structure or check for CVD divergences on retests
- [01:47] Swing failure pattern trade setup
  - Occurs at a major higher timeframe level
  - Requires order flow confirmation
- [02:12] Failed auction trade setup
  - Price trades above a higher timeframe level for several hours or days before coming back below it
- [02:31] Mini range entry trigger
  - Based on lower timeframe ranges
  - Example: Waiting for a swing failure pattern of a lower timeframe range to enter a long trade
- [03:31] Market structure change entry trigger
  - Identifying lower highs and lower lows on lower timeframes (e.g., 5-minute chart)
  - Entering short trades when a lower low forms, with stop loss above the high
  - Entering long trades when a higher low forms, with stop loss below the low
  - Least preferred method due to wider stop loss placement
- [05:06] Retest entry trigger
  - Occurs when price moves into a significant level, rejects, and then retests the level
  - Look for divergences on CVD and trapped longs/shorts
  - Example: Short trade setup with bearish divergences and trapped longs on the retest
  - Example: Long trade setup with bullish divergences and trapped shorts on the retest
- [06:32] When not to trade a retest or market structure change
  - Open interest increases, high positive delta, and new longs coming in on a pullback indicate strength and potential continuation
- [07:13] Adding confluence to entry triggers
  - Front-running a key level and looking for a mini range fake-out provides a high-probability entry
- [07:45] Be okay with missing trades
  - Better to wait for a good entry trigger than to enter without a plan or reason
- [07:58] Announcement of an upcoming live stream covering the session and how to submit questions

By mastering these entry triggers and applying them in conjunction with higher timeframe analysis, traders can improve their timing and increase the probability of successful trades. Remember to always wait for a clear entry trigger and have a well-defined plan before entering any trade.
