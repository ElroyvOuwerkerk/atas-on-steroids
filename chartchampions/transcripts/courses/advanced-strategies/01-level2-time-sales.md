# Level 2 & Time and Sales

## Portal Summary & Highlights

In this comprehensive lesson, we dive deep into Level 2 and Time & Sales trading tools, essential components for understanding market depth and order flow. We explore how professional and institutional traders use the order book to identify support and resistance levels, gauge market sentiment, and make more informed trading decisions. Through live trade demonstrations, we'll see exactly how to implement these tools for scalping strategies with defined risk-reward setups.
