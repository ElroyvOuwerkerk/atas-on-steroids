# Locking Profits vs Flipping the Trade

## Portal Summary & Highlights

In this video, the presenter discusses two strategies for managing profitable trades: locking in profits and flipping the trade. The importance of having a hard rule set for taking profit is emphasized, and the concept of flipping a trade by opening a short position at resistance levels is introduced as an alternative to closing a large portion of a profitable long position.

### Timestamps:

- [00:30] The utility of locking in profits is discussed, highlighting how many traders fail by never taking profits and "round-tripping" their trades, which means letting a profitable trade come back to the entry point or stop-loss level.
- [01:25] The fear of closing a trade too early and missing out on potential profits is identified as a common emotional hurdle for traders.
- [01:46] The implementation of a hard rule set for taking profit is discussed, with the presenter sharing their personal take profit one level, which ranges from 2% to 15% depending on the context of the trade.
- [02:37] The concept of flipping the trade is introduced as an alternative to taking multiple take profit levels. This involves opening a short position at resistance levels after a significant move up, while keeping the original long position open.
- [04:00] The psychological advantage of flipping the trade is explained, as it allows the trader to be fully hedged and relaxed during a pullback, with the potential for the long or short position to continue running for profits.
- [05:52] The presenter suggests that flipping the trade works best for day swing trading, where the trader has an entry from the low of the day and can open a simultaneous short trade at key resistance levels.
- [06:47] The strategy of turning scalp trades into swing trades by locking in a short position at take profit levels is discussed as a tip and trick.
- [07:31] The importance of being content with locking in profits, even if the trade continues to move in the intended direction, is emphasized as a psychological battle that traders must overcome.
- [08:31] The presenter shares their strategy of trailing up the stop-loss on a long position using market structure (two higher lows back on a medium-term timeframe) after a significant rise in price.

By implementing these strategies and maintaining a positive mindset, traders can effectively manage their profitable trades and secure gains while still allowing for the potential of larger profits. Viewers are encouraged to ask questions in the Discord for further clarification on these concepts.
