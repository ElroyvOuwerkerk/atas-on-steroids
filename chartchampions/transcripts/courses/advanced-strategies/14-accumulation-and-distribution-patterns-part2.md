# Accumulation and Distribution Patterns: Part 2

## Portal Summary & Highlights

In this comprehensive session, we dive deep into distribution patterns - the bearish counterpart to accumulation patterns covered in Part 1. We explore how these patterns form after extended uptrends when price reaches significant resistance levels, and crucially, why distribution typically occurs much faster than accumulation. The session covers all five phases of distribution (A through E), key price action events like the Buying Climax, Upthrust, and Upthrust After Distribution, along with specific statistics and order flow analysis techniques to identify high-probability short entries.

- [13:24] Automatic Reaction (AR) ¥ Sell-off fueled by late longs getting liquidated ¥ Open interest typically decreasing or staying level ¥ The bottom of this sell-off establishes the range low ¥ If open interest increases on the move down, it likely won't form a trading range
- [14:49] Phase B: Secondary Test, Sign of Weakness, and Upthrust ¥ Secondary Test (ST): Retests the buying climax area to test supply/demand ¥ On rallies: Want to see low volume and decreasing open interest ¥ On sell-offs: Want to see higher volume ¥ Secondary tests can appear in different locations - not always in Phase B

- [21:41] Phase C: Upthrust After Distribution (UTAD) and Test ¥ Final move above range high to stop out early shorts ¥ Should occur on low volume and decreasing open interest ¥ Key statistic: 42% of the time, this final push shows increasing volume and OI, creating trap longs ¥ Takes out the high for the third time - maximum deception
