# 3rd Touch

## Portal Summary & Highlights

We also have cheat sheets available for this topic which can be downloaded as a high-res PDF via our vault section here : https://portal.chartchampions.com/vault - download is titled ‘Liquidity’

This video provides a detailed explanation of the Third Touch setup, a trading strategy that involves identifying three key pivots to enter a long or short trade. The video covers the utility, implementation, and tips for successfully applying this setup, emphasizing the importance of confirming the setup with trapped traders, high volume, and CVD divergences.

### Timestamps:

- [00:00:26] Utility of the Third Touch setup:
  - Pivot 1: First important low (long setup) or high (short setup)
  - Pivot 2: Swing failure pattern or failed auction of Pivot 1
  - Pivot 3: First retest of the original Pivot 1
- [00:01:26] Guidelines for the setup:
  - Pivot 2: Look for trapped longs and high volume
  - Pivot 3: Look for bearish (short) or bullish (long) CVD on the retest
  - Pivot 1: Ideally formed off a bounce or rejection from a key support/resistance level
- [00:02:41] Implementation of the Third Touch setup:
  - Price tests the previous high (Pivot 1) before the swing failure pattern
  - Price rejects from the secondary test of the first high
  - Execute trade with stop loss above the highest high of the three pivots (Pivot 2)
- [00:04:15] Take profit options:
  - Fixed range of the current range
  - Range point of control
  - Last major low or high
  - Composite control (CC) or session VWAP
- [00:05:52] Adapting the setup for different market conditions:
  - Don't be afraid to take a valid long setup in a bear market
  - Adjust take profit to a closer level (e.g., session VWAP or last high on a 1-5 minute chart)
- [00:06:26] Tips and tricks:
  - Look for trapped traders and high volume on Pivot 2
  - Wait for a lower timeframe market structure change to confirm entry after a visual rejection
  - The setup works best at key levels of support and resistance
  - If invalidated, look for a Three Drives pattern or another swing failure pattern
- [00:09:05] Additional confluence: Pivot 3 retesting Pivot 1 near the point of control of the swing failure pattern candle
- [00:09:49] Note: Three Drives pattern may not always form after invalidation
