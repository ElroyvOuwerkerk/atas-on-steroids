# If One Then Two

## Portal Summary & Highlights

We also have cheat sheets available for this topic which can be downloaded as a high-res PDF via our vault section here : https://portal.chartchampions.com/vault - download is titled ‘If One then Two’

The video teaches the "If 1, Then 2" trading strategy, which involves identifying key liquidity areas where buy or sell stops are likely to be triggered, and then trading the expected move in the opposite direction. The strategy can be applied across various timeframes and markets, helping traders stick to a well-defined plan. timestamps:

### Timestamps

- [00:34] Explains the utility of the "If 1, Then 2" strategy, which is based on trading assets reaching an area of liquidity before the next move happens in the opposite direction. The pattern occurs in both lower and higher timeframes, making it suitable for day trading and swing trading.
- [02:14] Outlines the steps to implement the strategy:
  - Find the daily or weekly bias by identifying key swing highs or lows.
  - Determine where buy and sell stops are likely to be on both sides of the internal range.
  - Build a plan based on where the market came from, current price location, likely price direction, and invalidation level.
- [05:13] Presents the first implementation example using Bitcoin on a higher timeframe, demonstrating how the strategy captures a long trade by following the "If 1, Then 2" plan.
- [10:16] Shows the second implementation example using Bitcoin on a lower timeframe, illustrating how the strategy is applied in a downtrend, targeting sell stops below the low.
- [12:17] Provides the third implementation example using the ES futures on a higher timeframe, highlighting how the strategy can be used for swing trading by identifying multiple swing lows and targeting the highest liquidity level.
- [16:40] Demonstrates the fourth implementation example using the ES futures on a one-minute timeframe, showing how the strategy is applied in a downtrend by taking sell stops first, then buy stops, and continuing lower.
- [18:29] Offers tips and tricks, such as using the high and low indicator to determine the daily bias, choosing to take only shorts in a downtrend or longs in an uptrend, and utilizing the Attis liquidity template to identify untapped swing highs and lows.

By mastering the "If 1, Then 2" strategy and applying it consistently, traders can improve their chances of success in various market conditions and timeframes. The key is to stick to the plan, let the trade come to you, and manage risk appropriately.
