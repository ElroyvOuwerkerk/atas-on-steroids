# Frontruns

## Portal Summary & Highlights

We also have cheat sheets available for this topic which can be downloaded as a high-res PDF via our vault section here : https://portal.chartchampions.com/vault - download is titled ‘Frontruns’

This video discusses how to identify when a key support or resistance level is temporarily "front run" and the high probability that the level will still be hit within the next 7 days in a bear market (92% for support, 81% for resistance). The presenter shares tips on using order flow analysis to confirm a front run and plan trades accordingly.
