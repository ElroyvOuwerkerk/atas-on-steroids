# CCTR Strategy

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This video provides a detailed walkthrough of the CCTR (Candle Close Trend Reversal) scalping strategy applied to Bitcoin trading. Viewers will learn precise entry and exit techniques using average candle range (ACR) for take profits, strict stop-loss management, and how to combine multiple confluences like open interest, CVD, and order flow for high-probability scalp trades that typically yield $100-$300 moves on Bitcoin.

### Detailed Timestamps

- [04:34] Exit Strategy Explained: Use 50% take profit at the 5-minute ACR (average candle range), typically $100-$150 for Bitcoin. TP2 at the 10-minute ACR around $200-$300 depending on volatility. Always collapse the trade at TP2.
- [05:36] Using Market Levels for Take Profits: If higher timeframe levels (daily open, previous day high/low, equilibrium) are nearby, use them as TPs. Allow a small buffer above/below previous day highs/lows since price often takes liquidity before reversing.
- [06:27] Critical Stop-Loss Rule: Always, always, always move stop loss to break-even immediately after TP1 hits. No questions, strict rules. This is non-negotiable.
- [06:42] Extending Trades on Bigger Levels: If a higher timeframe level is being targeted, allow the trade to run longer as a day trade. However, the stop loss stays right below the wick because this is a CCTR scalp, not a swing trade.
- [07:21] Trade Logging Tip: Take many screenshots in sequence with annotations. Trades happen very fast - sometimes 4-5 minutes or less. Expect to be stopped out many times - it's the cost of doing business.
- [07:56] Tip #1 - Smart Alerts: Use a 30-minute chart and set price alerts away from your level using the ACR (e.g., $300 buffer). This lets you walk away and return when alerts trigger before price reaches your level.
- [08:46] Tip #2 - Discord Logging Server: Create a private Discord server called "log" for quick screenshot pasting. Saves significant time when reviewing trades in the evening for statistics.
- [09:09] Bullish CCTR Signal Anatomy: The tail must always be at the open side of the candle. Enter with a market order at the open of the second trending candle after the reversal signal. Stop loss goes literally just below the low - very tight range.
- [10:14] Bearish CCTR Signal: The tail must be above the candle. Stop loss goes above the wick. Entry on the second candle open down.
- [11:05] Why ATAS for Range Charts: ATAS produces more reliable, "true" trending candles with proper tick and USD values compared to ExoCharts. Each consolidation/reversal pattern is cleaner for the strategy.
- [11:42] Bearish CCTR Trade Example: Price rises to previous day POC plus monthly open. Aggressive longs on second attempt to break monthly open fail. Look for bearish divergence, session opens, and trapped longs at the top with high positive delta but no price movement upward.
- [13:05] Trade Execution Recap: OI up + price down = new shorts. TP below the low for $100 move, move stop to break-even, collapse trade at negative fib for additional ~$200 drop.
- [13:35] Bullish Trade Example: Time and price meeting at session opens, SFPs at recent lows with single prints showing buying interest, reclaim above VWAP, drop into NSBOC, bullish CVD around 10 million.
- [14:30] Screen Setup #1 (Left Monitor): Volume candles (5M/10M), CVD on higher timeframe, 30-minute and 5-minute charts, all higher timeframe levels (daily/monthly POCs, value areas), and rotating templates in the corner.
- [15:50] Screen Setup #2 (Main Monitor): Order book center, smart tape on right showing OI and aggregated orders (50K/30K minimum), TR Charts as trading terminal.
- [16:28] Stream Deck Hotkeys: Two dedicated buttons - one for short market, one for long market. Allows lightning-fast entries when the second candle prints without grabbing the mouse.
- [16:53] Low Timeframe Open Interest Advantage: On lower timeframes, OI prints quickly without waiting. Combined with footprint statistics, delta data, and three volume profiles (daily, weekly, monthly), gives complete picture.
- [18:00] ATAS Range Chart Note: Called "range" instead of "trend reversal" on ATAS. Important: keep the chart open continuously because OI resets when zooming/refreshing.
- [18:21] Live Trade Walkthrough: Wait for level + candle confirmation. Avoid trading without confluence. Use CVD divergence and CC (Candle Close) levels as triggers.
- [20:02] Trapped Trader Analysis: Use footprint to identify trapped longs (only 1.1M volume into a candle). When price breaks below their level, ride the cascade down for TP1 ($100) and TP2.
- [21:25] Reverse Setup: After taking shorts, look for new setups forming with CVD on your side. Avoid longs in oversold territory - look for shorts at ascending triangle resistance.
- [23:46] Final Strategy Principles: Strive for consistency with a solid plan and strict rules. Process: Ready ? Choose level ? Set plan ? Trade plan ? Rinse and repeat. You can't beat the Fed printing dollars, but you can print your own with CCTR.
- [24:29] Advanced Strategy Warning: CCTR is very advanced. Study the modules on order flow, open interest, CVD, and delta to be properly prepared before trading this strategy.

Take action: Set up your charting platform with the recommended templates, practice identifying CCTR signals on historical Bitcoin data, and start with small positions to internalize the strict stop-loss and break-even rules. Remember - consistency comes from discipline, not from winning every trade!
