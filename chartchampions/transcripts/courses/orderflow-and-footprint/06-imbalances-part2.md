# Imbalances Part 2

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session outlines a disciplined framework for using order flow imbalances and absorption in conjunction with pre-defined reference levels. We explore how patience, higher-timeframe technical context, and confirmation through volume and delta help filter out low-probability setups, with a practical walkthrough on evaluating whether current price action reflects absorption or simply low-volume drift.

### Highlights

- [00:00] Introduction to trading imbalances only in relation to reference levels Ñ the pre-identified areas from higher-timeframe analysis that define where trades are considered.
- [00:25] The importance of asking whether a high probability setup truly exists, or if we are trading out of boredom. Trading should be approached as a business rather than a hobby, with a strict rule set for entries.
- [01:26] Building the framework:
  - Mark up higher timeframes (e.g., 1H order blocks, weekly resistance).
  - Identify confluence regions above (for shorts) and below (for longs).
  - Everything between confluence zones is a no-trade zone.
- [02:56] Imbalances are only relevant when price interacts with a reference point. Observing imbalances in the middle of a range provides no edge.
- [03:52] Two execution styles at a reference level:
  - Aggressive: Enter on evidence of absorption without waiting for structure.
  - Confirmation-based: Wait for a structural signal after absorption is visible.
- [04:59] Defining absorption: A rounded bottom with heavy negative delta and strong selling imbalances into the lows, while price fails to break down Ñ suggesting large limit buyers are absorbing market sell orders.
- [05:47] The value of footprint charts: they provide evidence of participation rather than assumption. Selling imbalances typically use thresholds around 400% or 61.8%.
- [06:18] Imbalances alone are insufficient. Base technical analysis Ñ channels, monthly levels, Fibonacci Ñ must be applied first to establish valid reference zones.
- [07:01] Live chart evaluation: volume tapering from 900M ? 700M ? 400M into the lows indicates low participation, not true absorption. Light footprint boxes visually confirm diminishing activity.
- [08:55] Conclusion on the current setup: no edge in buying now due to insufficient absorption. However, repeated selling imbalances at the lows create the potential for trapped shorts.
- [09:25] Alternative entry plan Ñ reclaim setup:
  - Wait for price to reclaim the reference level (9,425).
  - Look for an increase in volume on the reclaim (e.g., ~70M).
  - Require buying imbalances and strong positive delta as confirmation.
  - Trade target: return to the highs, driven by trapped short liquidation.

We are encouraged to apply this framework with patience, waiting only for setups where reference levels, volume, delta, and imbalance evidence align, and to manage risk carefully when executing.
