# Volume Part 5

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This video dives into session volume profile trading concepts, focusing on how to use previous day's value area highs, value area lows, and points of control as critical support and resistance levels. The key strategy highlighted is identifying naked points of control (untapped POCs from previous sessions) and combining them with other confluences to set high-probability bid orders, particularly for intraday traders in the 24-hour crypto markets.

### Detailed Timestamps:

- [00:00] Introduction to session volume as a tool for identifying pivotal value area lows, value area highs, and points of control from each trading session. For crypto's 24-hour market, daily periods are the primary focus.
- [00:29] Pre-trading checklist tip: Before starting any trading day, write down on paper the previous day's value area low, point of control, and value area high. These three levels are critically important, especially for day traders.
- [01:09] Additional important levels include previous week's value area low, point of control, and value area high, though day traders should primarily focus on previous day levels.
- [01:23] Introduction to naked points of control Ð a POC from a previous session that has not yet been retested. Generally provides a bounce on first approach, but always requires additional confluence before taking a trade.
- [02:19] Why naked POCs work as strong support/resistance:
  - POCs represent areas of highest transaction volume
  - When price breaks away, ~95% of traders typically get the direction wrong
  - Underwater traders want to exit at break-even when price returns
  - Combined with new traders entering at the level, this creates "double the strength"
- [03:53] Emphasis on first touch only Ð the first retest is the strongest. Don't trade naked POCs on the second or third retests.
- [04:12] Favorite setup: Naked point of control combined with the CC (Confluence Cluster) on weekly or monthly levels. These setups occur roughly once a week on intraday charts.
- [05:25] Pre-setting bids vs. waiting for confirmation: Recommendation to test waiting for reactions over the next 10 trades. Place an alert at the level, wait for rejection, then enter for higher-probability setups.
- [06:39] Personal approach: ~90% of trades are taken after waiting for reaction, not from pre-set bids. However, full-time traders may need to pre-set due to time constraints.
- [07:03] Live chart example: Walking through how price reacts off previous day's value area high, finds support at previous day's POC, and reclaims levels for continued bullish movement.
- [07:57] Brief mention of double distribution days Ð when price creates two separate distribution areas with single prints between them, requiring TPO charts for proper identification.
- [08:48] Detailed walkthrough showing repeated bounces between previous day's POC, value area high, and developing day's POC Ð demonstrating how these levels act as a roadmap for price action.
- [10:25] Tool recommendation: Exocharts is highlighted as beginner-friendly for automatically marking developing day and previous day levels (POC, VAH, VAL) directly on the chart.
- [11:50] Intraday setup example: A naked POC was touched for the first time, producing a $200 rejection Ð ideal for traders with pre-set bids. The second touch broke through to ~$9K.
- [12:42] Short setup example: An extended previous day's POC aligned with the CC creates a high-probability scalp short opportunity, with price reacting at this confluence before continuing higher.
- [13:46] Key takeaways summary:
  - Point 1: Use previous day's VAH, VAL, and POC for intraday support/resistance
  - Point 2: If price trades above a value area then accepts back into the VAH, there's an 80% probability of trading back down to the value area low
  - Point 3: Pre-set bids on naked points of control with additional confluence Ð the first touch has very strong probability of bouncing

Start implementing these volume profile concepts by marking the key previous day levels before each trading session, and practice waiting for reactions at confluence zones to develop more disciplined, high-probability trade entries. Combine naked points of control with other confluences for some of the most powerful setups in your trading arsenal!
