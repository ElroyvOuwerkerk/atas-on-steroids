# Volume Part 2 (OBV)

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session focuses on On-Balance Volume (OBV) as an educational tool for interpreting volume dynamics within price action. We explore how OBV can serve as a leading indicator, highlight divergences at market tops and bottoms, confirm healthy trends, and signal potential breakouts or breakdowns before they materialize in price.

### Highlights

- [00:00] Introduction to On-Balance Volume (OBV) and why it is viewed as a factual measure of volume rather than a conventional price or momentum indicator.
- [00:33] The importance of volume in trading:
  - Volume helps determine whether a move is healthy or unhealthy.
  - Volume can hint at continuation or reversal.
- [01:00] Practical configuration notes:
  - Default settings are sufficient.
  - The absolute OBV number is not meaningful Ñ focus on patterns and divergences.
- [01:55] Core principle: Volume leads price. OBV may break support or resistance before price does, providing early signals.
- [02:30] Best practice reminder: OBV should always be used in confluence with other tools, never in isolation.
- [02:47] Multiple applications of OBV:
  - Identifying potential market tops.
  - Confirming breakouts.
  - Anticipating directional breaks from ranges.
  - Spotting divergences.
- [03:46] Example of OBV leading price: OBV breaks resistance before price confirms, suggesting an upcoming upside break.
- [04:39] Characteristics of a healthy uptrend: price and OBV making new highs together, indicating fresh participation and sustained momentum.
- [05:39] Bearish divergence explained: price prints a new high while OBV forms a lower high, signaling a potential reversal zone.
- [06:30] Comparing OBV divergences to RSI divergences Ñ same methodology, often more informative via OBV.
- [06:57] Case study of OBV leading price during a descending triangle breakdown:
  - Price moved sideways while OBV declined sharply.
  - This divergence between flat price and falling OBV suggested imminent downside.
- [08:33] Using patterns within OBV (triangles, trendlines) as early indicators Ñ a break of a pattern on OBV may precede the equivalent price move.
- [09:35] Combining OBV with directional bias: when a bearish bias is already present, OBV breaking down adds strong confirmation.
- [09:55] Applying OBV in range-bound markets:
  - Consolidation above support with OBV weakening increases the probability of a breakdown.
  - Acknowledgement that OBV is not infallible and can produce false signals.

We encourage reviewing these OBV concepts carefully and applying them alongside other confirmation tools and sound risk management practices.
