# Anchored VWAP

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session introduces the Anchored VWAP tool, explaining how it differs from the standard session VWAP by allowing a manually selected starting point to monitor volume-weighted average price from a specific pivot. We learn how to identify effective anchor points such as major highs, lows, trend starts, and range boundaries, and how anchored VWAPs can act as dynamic support or resistance. The lesson also emphasizes the importance of using anchored VWAP in confluence with other technical tools rather than in isolation.

### Highlights

- [00:00] Introduction to the Anchored VWAP tool, including its structure: utility, implementation, and tips and tricks.
- [00:46] Key difference between the session VWAP (automatic, resets each session) and the anchored VWAP (manual, starts from a chosen pivot). The anchored VWAP tracks volume from a user-defined point and acts as a dynamic support or resistance line.
- [01:30] Guidance on choosing effective anchor points: We then wait for a reaction at the anchored VWAP before considering a trade.
  - Major highs or lows
  - Start of a trend
  - Candles where price breaks out of a range
- [01:52] Example of anchoring the VWAP at the start of a 4-hour uptrend. On the pullback, price taps the anchored VWAP and produces a bounce, demonstrating its role as support.
- [02:49] Clarification that minor wicks or candles piercing the anchored VWAP do not invalidate it. The level is still considered valid as long as it broadly contains price action as support or resistance.
- [03:45] Sign of weakness: when price loses the anchored VWAP of an uptrend and flips it into resistance, this can be an early signal of a potential trend change.
- [04:28] Using anchored VWAPs in confluence. Example of three anchored VWAPs Ñ from the start of the uptrend, start of the range, and last high of the range Ñ all aligning at the same zone, creating a stronger support area.
- [05:26] Review of valid anchor locations within a range: Alignment of multiple anchored VWAPs often produces a stronger reaction.
  - Start of a local trend
  - Last high of the current range
  - Start of the overall range
- [05:49] Combining anchored VWAP with other tools such as a fractal for added confluence. Example shown where an anchored VWAP aligned precisely with a fractal target zone, producing a notable bounce.
- [06:28] Tips and tricks:
  - Never trade an anchored VWAP in isolation Ñ require confluence with other tools.
  - The tool can be applied on any timeframe, from 1-minute to weekly and above.
  - Place several anchored VWAPs and prioritize the one showing the most confluence with other technical levels.

We encourage reviewing these concepts carefully and applying them with sound risk management, using anchored VWAP as a supporting tool within a broader confluence-based framework.
