# Daily Open Strategy

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session explores how to trade daily opens in cryptocurrency markets by analyzing the initial balance Ñ the first hour of trading after the daily open at midnight UTC. We review three distinct open types (Open Drive, Open Test Drive, and Open Rejection Reversal), how to identify each through price action and volume, and how each setup provides a defined entry trigger and invalidation. We also learn how recognizing the open type helps us predict whether the remaining 23 hours of the day are likely to be trending or range-bound.

### Highlights

- [00:00] Introduction to daily opens as a reactive trading concept Ñ strategies vary depending on how the opening hour develops.
- [01:03] Importance of monitoring the first hour of the daily open, with theory originating from stock market trading but applied to crypto's 24-hour market.
- [01:49] Key questions to review after the first hour:
  - Did price make a strong directional move?
  - Was there significant volume?
  - Was the move trending or sideways?
  - Did the open occur within, above, or below the previous day's value area?
- [02:24] Two reactive approaches: trading retests of the daily open or trading with continued strength after a volume-backed impulsive move.
- [03:21] Definition of the initial balance Ñ the first hour of the trading day Ñ and how all three strategies are derived from this reference window.
- [03:53] The daily open occurs at midnight UTC globally. Because crypto trades 24/7, setups may take longer to resolve than in equity markets.
- [04:45] Open Drive setup:
  - Preferred when price opens outside the previous day's value area.
  - Characterized by an immediate, strong, volume-backed impulsive move off the open.
  - Indicates higher timeframe participants have taken the initiative.
  - Expectation: a trending day, not a fade or reversal.
- [06:50] Open Test Drive setup:
  - Starts slow with meandering, low-conviction price action.
  - Price loses the daily open level, retests it, then produces a volume injection.
  - Most commonly discussed open type in crypto education.
- [07:35] Open Rejection Reverse setup:
  - Weak open with no initial volume.
  - Price drifts toward a significant key reference level (value area, horizontal support/resistance, Fibonacci, weekly or monthly level).
  - A strong rejection at the key level drives price back through the daily open with volume.
- [09:01] Entry and invalidation logic for the Open Drive:
  - Entry: upon confirmation of initiative volume off the open.
  - Invalidation: price returning above the daily open; however, use nearby market structure (e.g., prior highs or an order block) as the actual stop placement rather than the open itself.
  - If structure is far, use a smaller position size to accommodate a wider stop.
- [11:17] Open Test Drive example and execution:
  - Recognized by choppy, sideways early action with wicks and no conviction.
  - Confirmation comes when price retests the open and begins one time framing away impulsively.
  - Entry trigger: the volume injection candle off the retest.
  - Invalidation: the low (or high) of the test candle Ñ not the open itself.
  - Targets must be determined via additional technical analysis (Fibonacci, Elliott waves, etc.).
- [14:43] Open Rejection Reversal execution:
  - Weak open; price seeks a predefined key level (e.g., previous day's value area high).
  - Entry trigger: price rejects the key level and moves back through the daily open with volume.
  - Invalidation: back above the key level.
  - Alternative: enter earlier directly at the key level for a tighter stop, instead of waiting for confirmation through the open.
- [16:28] Summary of the framework:
  - Use the initial balance (first 60 minutes) to predict the day's character.
  - Strong impulsive IB with volume ? expect a trending day.
  - Weak IB ? expect either a range-bound day or wait for a retest of the open / key level before trading.
- [19:02] Core actionable checklist:
  - Measure daily open strength Ñ directional vs. sideways.
  - Identify open location Ñ inside or outside previous value area.
  - Monitor for volume injection as entry confirmation.
  - Combine these elements to predict the day type and plan accordingly.
- [20:56] Closing concept: "Understanding the open type lets us trade the next 23 hours with more conviction." Recognition becomes intuitive with repeated screen time and study, reducing the need to consciously classify setups while trading.

We encourage reviewing these open-type frameworks carefully, backtesting them across multiple sessions, and applying them only with disciplined risk management and clearly defined invalidation levels.
