# Trendline Trading

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This video offers a comprehensive deep dive into trend line trading strategies, covering everything from basic trend line construction to advanced techniques like the three trend line fan break for identifying market reversals. Viewers will learn how to identify valid trend lines, when to enter trades on the third versus fourth touch, the importance of volume confirmation on breakouts, and how to use multi-timeframe analysis for higher probability entries.

### Detailed Timestamps:

- [00:00] Introduction to Trend Lines - Trend lines are one of the simplest tools in trading, formed by connecting a series of lows (uptrend) or highs (downtrend). The basic principle requires at least two touches to draw the line, with three touches creating a confirmed trend line.
- [00:47] The Three-Touch Confirmation Rule - A trend line becomes "confirmed" after three touches. If price breaks below this confirmed support line, traders should consider closing longs or entering shorts as the support has failed.
- [01:21] Trading the Third Touch vs. Fourth Touch - While most traders wait for the fourth touch as confirmation, the strategy here favors buying the third touch for better entry prices. Confluence is critical - never buy purely off a third touch without supporting factors.
- [02:32] Short Entry Criteria on Trend Line Breaks - The ideal short setup requires: 1. Price breaks down below the trend line 2. Back-test of the trend line as resistance 3. Break below the previous support low This sequence confirms the trend reversal.
- [03:05] Multi-Timeframe Approach for 4-Hour Traders - For 4-hour trend line traders, dropping to the 1-hour chart for entries works well. Wait for proper confirmation rather than jumping the gun, especially when the trend line aligns with strong horizontal support.
- [04:49] Wicks vs. Candle Closes for Trend Lines - Personal preference is to use wicks consistently. The key principle: be consistent - don't mix wicks and candle bodies. Choose one method and stick with it.
- [05:21] Volume Confirmation on Breakouts - Volume should increase significantly on trend line breaks, especially breakdowns. Look for volume nearly double the 20-period moving average as warning/confirmation signs.
- [06:08] Introduction to the Three Trend Line Fan Break - An underutilized strategy that works best on higher timeframes (4-hour, daily, weekly). Not recommended for scalping on 3-minute charts.
- [07:24] How the Three Fan Lines Form - The methodology: ¥ Line 1: Connects initial pivotal high to series of lower highs (downtrend) ¥ Line 2: After Line 1 breaks, connects new high to subsequent highs ¥ Line 3: Forms after Line 2 breaks; when broken, confirms full trend reversal
- [09:07] Trend Reversal Confirmation - Breaking through all three trend lines is a strong confirmation of trend reversal, signaling a shift from bear to bull market (or vice versa). After this confirmation, focus on buying dips rather than seeking shorts.
- [10:00] Log Scale Considerations - Log scale is generally not used for the fan break method. Log scale is more relevant when analyzing many years of historical data on highly volatile assets.
- [10:58] Live ETH Daily Chart Example - Real-world application showing two of three fan lines already formed on Ethereum's daily chart, with explanation of what conditions would complete the pattern and signal a long entry.
- [13:36] Refining Trend Line Selection - Multiple valid trend lines can exist; better-drawn trend lines often provide earlier entry points and capture more of the move. Always look for the trend line with the most respected touches.
- [15:45] The 45-Degree Trend Line (Gann Theory) - While not personally used, this concept is worth knowing: ¥ A 45-degree slope represents a healthy, sustainable trend ¥ Movement above 45 degrees indicates a parabolic advance - unsustainable and prone to devastating reversals ¥ Useful for assessing trend strength and risk

Apply these trend line strategies to your own chart analysis, but always remember to incorporate confluence and volume confirmation for the highest probability setups. Start practicing the three fan line technique on higher timeframes to better identify major trend reversals!
