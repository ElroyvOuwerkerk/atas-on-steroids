# Pivot Points Strategy

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session provides a comprehensive introduction to Pivot Points as a trading tool, covering how to set them up on TradingView, the different pivot calculation types (with a focus on Fibonacci Pivots), and how to apply them across intraday and swing trading timeframes. We explore how pivot levels function as support, resistance, entry points, take-profit targets, and invalidation levels, including the use of Chinese exchange pivots as naked pivot levels that attract price.

### Highlights

- [00:00] Introduction to Pivot Points:
  - Pivot points can be used as targets for a move.
  - They provide invalidation levels (stop losses) for setups.
  - An entire strategy can be built around pivots, offering entries, targets, and stop placements.
- [00:54] Setting Up Pivot Points on TradingView:
  - Open Indicators ? Built-In ? search "Pivot Points Standard".
  - Choose between Traditional, Fibonacci, Woody, Classic, DM, or Camarilla types.
  - Fibonacci pivots pair well with other Fibonacci-based tools for added confluence.
- [02:25] Live Example on Ethereum: Price finds support at S1, moves up to R1, breaks through, and extends to R2, demonstrating how pivots act as both support and resistance.
- [03:36] Core Pivot Structure Explained:
  - P = central pivot
  - R1, R2 = resistance levels above the pivot (used as take-profit targets on longs)
  - S1, S2 = support levels below the pivot (used as take-profit targets on shorts)
- [04:33] Importance of Confluence: Trading pivots alone is insufficient. Combine them with Fibonacci extensions, higher-timeframe levels, or confluence zones to improve probability.
- [05:00] Example of Overextension Trade: If price reaches R2 quickly with multiple confluences, it may act as resistance for a short back to R1 or P.
- [06:39] Timeframes to Use:
  - 15-minute chart ? Intraday pivots (reset at every daily open)
  - 1-hour chart ? Swing trade pivots (reset at every Monday open)
- [08:37] Comparing Pivot Types: Fibonacci, Traditional, Woody, and Classic place the central pivot at slightly different levels due to varying calculation formulas.
- [09:53] Textbook Intraday Example on Bitcoin:
  - Rejection at daily open pivot
  - Backtest of pivot as resistance
  - Move down to S1 ? support in a bull market context
  - Long entry at S1 with stop below S2
  - Targets at R1 and R2
- [11:47] Extending Targets: If R2 breaks, R3 can be added for the next target; S3 serves the same purpose on the downside.
- [12:54] Swing Trader's Perspective (1H Chart): Monday open pivot acts as weekly support in a bullish structure, with R1 and R2 serving as weekly upside targets.
- [15:30] Chinese Exchange Pivots (OKCoin / UOB):
  - Based on Chinese exchange sessions.
  - Become visible around 4 PM UK time, functioning like a naked point of control.
  - Price is often drawn toward these untested levels, offering potential support/resistance reactions.
- [17:00] Live Chinese Pivot Example: Price gravitated toward a naked pivot at ~47,700 and produced a strong bounce, preceding a significant upside move.
- [19:30] Recap of Chinese Pivots: Use on the 15-minute chart, released at 4 PM UK time, and treat as high-probability support/resistance zones.
- [26:00] Interpreting the Pivot (P):
  - Above P ? bullish bias
  - Below P ? bearish bias
  - Broken pivots often flip from resistance to support (or vice versa).
- [30:00] Building Confluence with Pivots:
  - Horizontal support/resistance levels
  - Fibonacci retracements and 1:1 extensions aligning with R1/R2
  - Momentum signals such as bearish divergence at R2/R3
- [31:00] Important Context Warning: Price hitting R2 or R3 does not guarantee a return to P. Reversal probability depends on speed of move, confluences, divergences, and market structure shifts.
- [33:19] Combining Pivots with Daily Open Strategies: Pivots can enhance open-drive, open-test-drive, and open-rejection-reversal setups by providing clear reference levels.
- [36:50] Using Both Standard and Chinese Pivots: They operate on different sessions and can be combined as additional confluence rather than one replacing the other.
- [39:30] Session Summary:
  - Pivots offer support, resistance, and target levels.
  - 15-minute for intraday, 1-hour for swing trading.
  - Chinese naked pivots attract price and can produce strong reactions.
  - Always combine pivots with additional confluence rather than trading them in isolation.

We encourage traders to review these pivot concepts carefully, test them within their own methodology, and always apply strict risk management when integrating them into any trading plan.
