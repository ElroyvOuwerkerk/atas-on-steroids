# Swing Trading

## Portal Summary & Highlights

We also have cheat sheets available for this topic which can be downloaded as a high-res PDF via our vault section here : https://portal.chartchampions.com/vault - download is titled ‘Swing Trading’

This swing trading video provides an in-depth look at the utility, implementation, and tips for successful swing trading. The presenter highlights the benefits of swing trading for those with less free time and explains how it requires fewer trades but allows for well-planned entries. The video covers the preferred time frames, entry triggers, stop loss placement, and take profit targets for swing trades.

### Timestamps:

- [00:00:00] Introduction and legal trade disclaimer
- [00:00:18] Overview of the video structure: utility, implementation, and tips and tricks
- [00:00:33] Utility of swing trading
  - Great for those with less free time (full-time job, family, etc.)
  - More relaxed way of trading compared to scalping and day trading
  - Fewer trades but well-planned entries
- [00:01:55] Implementation of swing trading
  - Preferred time frames: 4-hour, monthly, and weekly for analysis; 30-minute for trade execution
  - Key tools: horizontals, NPOCs, and liquidity points
  - Best entry triggers: swing failure patterns (SFPs) and failed auctions
  - Setting alerts above/below liquidity levels for SFPs or back inside the range for failed auctions
  - Confirming reactions on the 30-minute chart before entering trades
- [00:05:37] Stop loss placement and take profit targets
  - Stop loss always above the SFP wick or the highest point of the failed auction
  - First target can be an intraday level (VWAP, SFP 2.0 target) or the value area high of the range
  - Second target can be a trailing stop loss or the range's value area low/major liquidity level
- [00:06:46] Visual examples of swing trading setups
  - Recognizing liquidity levels and setting alerts
  - Waiting for SFPs or failed auctions to trigger entries
  - Adjusting stop losses and taking profits based on market structure
- [00:11:34] Tips and tricks for swing trading success
  - Don't get too greedy on the first take profit (aim for 15-25%)
  - Consider context when deciding to move stop loss to entry or keep it at the original invalidation level
  - Don't worry if you miss an alert trade; there will always be another opportunity
  - Set alerts to ensure you don't miss planned trades
- [00:13:35] Conclusion and information about the upcoming live Q&A session

By mastering the skills taught in this video and other modules, you can develop a solid swing trading strategy that allows you to capitalize on well-planned trade opportunities while maintaining a balanced lifestyle. Remember to stay disciplined, manage your risk, and continually refine your approach as you gain experience in the markets.
