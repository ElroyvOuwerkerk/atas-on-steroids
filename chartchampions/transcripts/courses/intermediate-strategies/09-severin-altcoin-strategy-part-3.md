# Severin Altcoin Strategy Part 3

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session walks through a complete altcoin trading workflow, demonstrating how to build directional bias from Bitcoin and Ethereum before conducting in-depth analysis on individual altcoins. We review methods for identifying high-probability setups using high timeframe levels, fixed range volume profiles, confluences such as CCs, anchored VWAPs, and naked points of control, along with structured entry techniques tailored to swing and intraday trades.
