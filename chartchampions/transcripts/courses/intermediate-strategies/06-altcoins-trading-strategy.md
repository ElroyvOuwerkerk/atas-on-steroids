# Altcoins Trading Strategy

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session explains an approach to trading altcoins by focusing primarily on the BTC pair for analysis, even when executing trades on the USD or USDT pair. We learn why tracking the underlying BTC pair is essential for identifying outperformance, how to decide between trading pairs, and why limiting the number of altcoins traded can improve overall trade quality.

### Highlights

- [00:00] Introduction to the concept: performing analysis on the BTC pair while trading the USDT/USD pair.
- [01:02] Overview of the different altcoin trading pairs available (BTC, USDT, USD, ETH, etc.) and why only two pairs really matter:
  - The BTC pair Ñ mandatory for all altcoin traders.
  - The USDT/USD pair Ñ only if that is what you actually trade.
- [02:38] Explanation that the altcoin/BTC pair represents the underlying spot price of the altcoin relative to Bitcoin, making it the key chart to monitor.
- [03:13] Concept of relative outperformance: longing an altcoin against BTC only makes sense if the altcoin is expected to outperform Bitcoin.
- [04:27] Practical scenario: if LINK/BTC is at resistance but LINK/USDT is at support, buying the USDT pair may be a poor decision because Bitcoin is likely outperforming LINK Ñ making BTC the better long.
- [05:58] Key principle: it doesn't matter if you profit on the USDT pair if you could have made more by simply holding/trading Bitcoin.
- [06:39] Workflow: always complete BTC pair analysis first, then move on to the USD/USDT pair analysis.
- [06:56] Why this approach requires trading fewer altcoins Ñ each altcoin requires two charts (BTC pair + USDT pair), so 20 alts means managing 40 charts.
- [08:08] Example of conflicting signals: if BTC pair is expected to drop 14% while the USDT pair looks like a long, taking the long is risky because of the strong correlation to Bitcoin.
- [09:46] Real example using LINK: a 13% drop on the BTC pair correlates with break-even or small moves on the USDT pair, illustrating how BTC pair behavior dominates outcomes.
- [10:32] Summary of the methodology:
  - Always analyze the BTC pair, even when trading USD/USDT.
  - Trade fewer altcoins to remain realistic and manageable.
  - Aim for higher trade quality by dedicating more focus to each position.

We encourage reviewing these concepts carefully and applying them with proper risk management before incorporating them into any altcoin trading plan.
