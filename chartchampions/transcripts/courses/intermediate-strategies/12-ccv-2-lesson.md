# CCV 2.0

## Portal Summary & Highlights

Learn about CCV 2.0, an improved version of the popular CCV setup that addresses its limitations and increases win rate. Discover how CCV 2.0 uses a fake-out entry trigger to provide better risk-reward ratios and more reliable trade setups by combining the original CCV concept with swing failure patterns. We also have cheat sheets available for this topic which can be downloaded as a high-res PDF via our vault section here : https://portal.chartchampions.com/vault - download is titled ‘CCV 2.0 Setup’

- [00:59] Limitations of the original CCV setup - Difficulty with entry trigger and poor risk-reward ratio - Longing after a big move has already occurred
- [01:35] CCV 2.0 implementation - Wait for a normal CCV setup to form - Look for a fake-out of the current daily high (for short) or low (for long) - Enter trade on the fake-out, using it as a swing failure pattern or failed auction - Target the other side of the value area in that range
