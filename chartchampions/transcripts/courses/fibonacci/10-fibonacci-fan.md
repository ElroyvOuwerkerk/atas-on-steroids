# Fibonacci Fan

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session focuses on Fibonacci Speed Fans as a powerful but underutilized tool for identifying support, resistance, and the angle/time dynamics of price moves on macro timeframes. The session also reviews a recent Theta trade example built around a bull flag and 0.618 retracement confluence, and concludes with a bullish Bitcoin outlook based on weekly resistance reclaim, three drives pattern targeting ~$11K, and extended harmonic targets.

- [14:04] Key principle: when combined with Fibonacci retracements and Fibonacci time, all three tools create extremely strong confluence zones.
