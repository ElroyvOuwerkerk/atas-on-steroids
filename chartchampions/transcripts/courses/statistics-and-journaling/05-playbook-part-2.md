# Playbook: Part 2

## Portal Summary & Highlights

In this video, Rivalry shares his personal playbook setups for divergence plays and VWAP traps, focusing on index trading. He emphasizes the importance of understanding tier levels, one-time framing, and delta trends to identify high-probability trades. Rivalry also stresses the significance of taking profits and managing trades based on market structure.
