# Trading Journal (CCTR)

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This comprehensive video walks through the importance of maintaining a trading journal specifically designed for the CCTR (Candle Close Time Reversal) strategy, day trades, and swing trades. The presentation demonstrates how a well-structured journal helps identify trading habits, track strengths and weaknesses, and ensure consistent profitability over time. Viewers learn how to pre-plan trades by visualizing potential risk and reward in dollar and percentage terms before entering, while emphasizing the critical principle of risking 1 to make at least 2.
