# How to Record Statistics

## Portal Summary & Highlights

We also have cheat sheets available for this topic which can be downloaded as a high-res PDF via our vault section here : https://portal.chartchampions.com/vault - download is titled ‘How to Record Statistics’

This lesson focuses on the importance of recording trading statistics to build confidence and achieve consistency in trading. By tracking key metrics such as win rate, risk-reward ratio, drawdown, and number of trades, traders can make informed decisions based on probabilities and raw data. The video provides practical examples and an Excel file to help traders start tracking their own statistics.

### Timestamps:

- [00:00:00] Introduction and legal trade disclaimer
- [00:00:21] Utility of recording statistics: overcoming lack of confidence and building discipline
- [00:01:28] Key trading statistics to track: win rate, risk-reward, drawdown, number of trades
- [00:02:22] Consistency in recording data is crucial for achieving consistency in trading
- [00:02:46] Goal: 60-65% win rate with a 2:1 risk-reward ratio
- [00:03:19] Implementation: collecting statistics on a tick-based approach
  - Enter the level to collect data (e.g., daily open)
  - Record total ticks from the move away from the naked level (green)
  - Record total ticks through the stop loss level (red)
- [00:05:24] Statistics build over time, including average ticks, wins, losses, total trades, and win percentage
- [00:06:00] Another way to collect data: tracking weekly highs and lows
  - Use the provided Excel table to input daily highs and lows
  - Excel automatically highlights the highest high (green) and lowest low (red) for each week
- [00:08:49] Example of completed data collection for 20 weeks, showing percentages of highs and lows landing on specific days
- [00:09:14] Tips and tricks:
  - Use different stats for different tick sizes (e.g., BTC naked point of control)
  - Record session highs and lows using the high and low indicator on a 15-minute chart
  - Target a minimum 1:1 risk-reward ratio, with 2:1 preferred
  - Allow time between naked levels for higher probability trades
  - Add session times in stats for highs and lows
  - Risk the same equity per trade for consistent risk management
- [00:12:54] Encouragement to start collecting data now
- [00:13:02] Information about upcoming live Q&A stream and additional resources

By implementing the strategies and tips provided in this lesson, traders can build a solid foundation of statistical analysis to improve their trading confidence and performance. Start tracking your statistics today and let the data guide your trading decisions.
