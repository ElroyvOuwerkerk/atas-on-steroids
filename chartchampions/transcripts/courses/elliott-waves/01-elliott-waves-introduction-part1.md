# Elliott Waves: Introduction Part 1

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This video provides an introduction to Elliott Waves, a powerful tool for predicting future prices and targets in financial markets. The presenter explains the utility of Elliott Waves, their basic implementation, and offers valuable tips and tricks for traders. By mastering trading psychology and emotions, traders can effectively use Elliott Waves in conjunction with Fibonacci tools to make informed trading decisions.

### Timestamps:

- [00:00] Introduction and legal trade disclaimer
- [00:34] Utility of Elliott Waves
  - Best tool for predicting future prices and targets in any market
  - Uses previous price action and Fibonacci tools to predict the future
  - Can predict the date a move will happen using Fibonacci time
- [01:14] Requirements for being a good Elliott Wave trader
  - Mastering trading psychology and emotions
  - Buying on retracements and selling when the market is most bullish
  - Ability to switch from bullish to bearish quickly when required
  - Having two scenarios (bullish and bearish counts) is essential
- [02:22] Basic implementation of Elliott Waves
  - Consists of impulse waves (trend) and corrective waves (pullback)
  - Impulse wave has a five-wave structure (waves 1, 3, and 5)
  - Corrective wave has a three-wave structure (waves 2 and 4)
  - Each wave has a subset of waves inside it (fractal nature)
  - After a completed five-wave impulse, expect an ABC correction
- [05:18] Tips and tricks for using Elliott Waves
  - Can be used on any time frame from one month to one minute
  - Higher time frames (4 hour+) are preferred by the presenter
  - More time and data may be needed to confirm if a structure is impulsive or corrective
  - Every trader should have both a bullish and bearish count, trading only the preferred count at any one time
  - If the preferred count is invalidated, switch to the alternate count
  - All price action in Elliott Waves is fractal

By understanding and applying the concepts presented in this video, traders can leverage the power of Elliott Waves to make more informed and profitable trading decisions. Remember to always maintain both bullish and bearish wave counts, and be prepared to adapt your trading plan as market conditions change.
