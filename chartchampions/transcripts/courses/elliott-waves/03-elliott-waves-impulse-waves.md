# Elliott Waves: Impulse Waves

## Portal Summary & Highlights

### Timestamps

- [00:00] Introduction and legal disclaimer
- [00:18] Utility of Impulse Waves
  - Five-wave internal structure (waves 1, 3, and 5 are impulse waves)
  - Subwave structure within waves 1, 3, and 5
  - Fractal nature of price movements across different timeframes
- [01:34] Implementation of Impulse Waves
  - Start analysis on the highest timeframe (e.g., one-month chart)
  - Use log scale for charts with one or more years of data
  - Mark out each major high and low, looking for five major pivot points
- [02:38] Adjacent Wave Rule
  - Adjacent waves should be of the same price and time degree
  - The smaller of two adjacent waves should not be less than one-third of the larger wave in price and time
  - Examples of correct and incorrect wave counts based on the adjacent wave rule
- [05:59] Important Rules of Elliott Wave Impulse Waves
  - Wave 2 can never retrace all of wave 1
  - Wave 3 must be longer than wave 2
  - Wave 4 can never retrace all of wave 3
  - Wave 5 has to be over 38.2% of wave 4
  - Wave 3 can never be the shortest wave between waves 1, 3, and 5
- [07:30] Visualizing the 38.2% Rule
  - Invalid count: Wave 5 falling below the 38.2% Fibonacci retracement of wave 3 to 4
  - Valid count: Wave 5 ending above the 38.2% Fibonacci retracement of wave 3 to 4
  - Fifth wave failure: When wave 5 is shorter than wave 3 but still above the 38.2% retracement
- [10:00] Tips and Tricks
  - Use log scale for charts with one or more years of data or high volatility
  - Start on the one-month chart and work your way down through lower timeframes
  - Be aware that a suspected third wave impulse start can sometimes turn into a corrective zigzag pattern
- [11:20] Conclusion and invitation to ask questions during the live stream or via trading help on the website or Discord
