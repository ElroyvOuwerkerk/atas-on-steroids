# Elliott Waves: Channels

## Portal Summary & Highlights

### Timestamps:

- [00:00:28] Introduction to using parallel channels with Elliott Waves for targets and invalidations.
- [00:01:12] Implementation of channels for zigzag corrections:
  - First click: start of wave A
  - Second click: end of wave B
  - Third click: termination of wave A
  - Wave C should not end directly at the channel touch
- [00:02:45] Identifying complex corrections and the end of the corrective pattern using channel breakouts.
- [00:03:16] Creating horizontal channels for flat corrections.
- [00:04:00] Constructing channels for impulse wave 3 and wave 5:
  - Wave 3: first touch (start of wave 1), second touch (end of wave 2), third touch (end of wave 1)
  - Wave 5: first touch (end of wave 2), second touch (end of wave 4), third touch (end of wave 3)
- [00:05:28] Adjusting channel construction for extended wave 3 scenarios.
- [00:06:13] Using trendlines to identify the completion of waves:
  - 0-2 trendline: connects start of wave 1 to end of wave 2
  - 2-4 trendline: connects end of wave 2 to end of wave 4
- [00:07:03] 2-4 trendline break should occur in the same amount of time or less than the time consumed by wave 5.
- [00:09:06] Readjusting trendlines when waves extend beyond initial expectations.
- [00:10:34] Scenarios when the 2-4 trendline break takes longer than wave 5:
  - Terminal impulse pattern (ending diagonal)
  - Incorrectly formed 2-4 trendline due to unfinished wave 4
- [00:11:24] 0-B trendline for corrections (flats and zigzags):
  - Connects start of wave A to end of wave B
  - Breakout suggests the correction is likely finished
- [00:11:50] Breaking the B-D trendline in a triangle correction indicates the completion of the triangle.
- [00:13:40] Tips and tricks:
  - Perfect channeling generally indicates a corrective pattern
  - Use channels with confluence, not in isolation
  - 0-2 and 2-4 trendlines help identify the completion of corrective waves 2 and 4
  - 0-B trendline breakouts signal the end of zigzag corrections

By mastering the use of parallel channels and trendlines with Elliott Waves, traders can gain valuable insights into potential targets, invalidation levels, and the completion of various wave patterns. Incorporate these tools into your analysis, along with other confirming factors, to improve your wave counting and trading decisions.
