# Elliott Waves: My Way

## Portal Summary & Highlights

### Timestamps:

- [00:00] Introduction and legal trade disclaimer
- [00:24] Overview of the video structure: utility, implementation, and tips and tricks
- [01:08] Utility: Monitoring new patterns and probabilities via recording statistics - Emphasizes the importance of adapting to new patterns, even if they deviate from standard Elliott Wave rules - Example: Triangles forming in wave 2, which is traditionally not allowed
- [02:29] Implementation: How the trader uses Elliott Waves - Primarily on daily and weekly timeframes, without the need for lower timeframe validation - Journals and records statistics on all trade combinations - Uses Fibonacci extensions, expansions, and negative fibs for all-time high targets - Uses Fibonacci extensions, horizontals, and liquidity for internal wave targets - Willing to take trade setups even if they don't follow Elliott Wave rules, then records statistics
- [03:58] Tips and tricks - Beginners to intermediate traders should follow the rules and guidelines - Advanced traders can start trading off the rule set and recording statistics on new patterns - The trader uses Elliott Waves as a higher timeframe target tool, not a primary trading tool - Confluence is always required to take a trade setup - Lower timeframe sub-wave counts are not crucial if the higher timeframe count is valid
- [05:17] Expanding on going off the rules - Example: Trading a flat pattern with a 3-3-3 sub-wave structure, despite the traditional 3-3-5 rule - Questioning the relevance of old rules in modern markets, e.g., cryptocurrency
- [06:06] The importance of making profits over following rules - The trader emphasizes the goal of making profits, not just adhering to a rule set - The need for a large dataset (1000+ trades) and a high win rate (75%+) to validate new patterns
- [07:13] Elaborating on sub-wave structure and the upcoming live charting sessions - Rules are helpful but can be broken at times, leading to new patterns and rules based on statistics - Sub-wave structure validation on lower timeframes is not always necessary for the trader's approach
