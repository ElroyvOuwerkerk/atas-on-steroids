# Elliott Waves: Fibonacci Relationships

## Portal Summary & Highlights

### Timestamps

- [00:00] Introduction to the video on Fibonacci relationships in Elliott Waves
- [00:22] Utility of Fibonacci in Elliott Waves:
  - Fibonacci time, extensions, and retracements are more powerful when used alongside Elliott Waves
  - Each wave has a relationship with Fibonacci, which can be used to predict future price action
- [00:58] Relationships between waves 1, 3, and 5 in an impulse:
  - Two unextended waves normally relate by 61.8% or 100% in time and price
  - Wave 3 must be more than 161.8% of wave 1 if it's the extended wave
  - When wave 5 is the extension, wave 4 frequently retraces 50-66% of wave 3
- [03:10] Wave 5 extension:
  - If wave 5 is the extended wave, it will generally be 161.8% of the move from the start of wave 1 to the end of wave 3
  - Use the Fibonacci extension tool to find the target for wave 5
- [04:01] Wave 1 relationships with waves 3 and 5:
  - Wave 3 should be at least a 1.618 extension of wave 1
  - Wave 5 should be a 1-to-1 extension of wave 1
- [05:26] Defining an extended wave:
  - The longest wave in the sequence (waves 1, 3, and 5)
  - Should be at least 161.8% of the next longest wave
- [06:04] Retracements in waves 2 and 4:
  - Wave 2 commonly retraces 66% (CC zone) of wave 1
  - Wave 4 commonly retraces 38.2% to 50% of wave 3
- [06:23] Fibonacci relationships in flats:
  - After a strong B wave, look for a 138.2% or 161.8% expansion
  - Wave C is normally equal to wave A (1-to-1, 161.8%, or 61.8% of wave A)
- [08:29] Fibonacci relationships in zigzags:
  - Wave C is normally 100% the length of wave A, extended from the end of wave B
  - Maximum wave C can go is 2.618 times the length of wave A
- [08:47] Fibonacci relationships in triangles:
  - Waves A, C, and E generally relate by 66% (CC zone)
- [10:24] Tips and tricks summary:
  - Wave 3 must be more than 1.618% of wave 1 if it's the extended wave
  - Wave 5 should be at least 100% the length of wave 1 and take 61.8% of the time it took to form wave 1
