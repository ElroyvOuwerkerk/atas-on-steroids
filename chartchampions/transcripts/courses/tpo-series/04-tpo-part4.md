# TPO Part 4

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session introduces an all-in-one charting template that combines the TPO profile, volume profile, and candlesticks into a single view. We learn how to interpret daily statistics such as time and volume point of control percentages, total delta, rotation factor, initial balance levels, and average candle range, and how to use discrepancies between time and volume to identify potential fade opportunities and directional bias.

### Highlights

- [00:00] Introduction to the all-in-one template combining TPO, volume profile, and candlesticks for a complete daily view of market structure.
- [00:57] Overview of the daily statistics block displayed above each TPO profile, which updates intraday and finalizes at session close for statistical analysis.
- [01:09] Explanation of A/B TPO percentages:
  - Shows the percentage of time spent above or below the time point of control.
  - Useful for gauging where the session's activity has been concentrated relative to fair value.
- [01:49] Above/Below Volume percentages apply the same concept to the volume point of control, and the referenced POC can be toggled in settings between time-based and volume-based.
- [02:37] Definitions of further daily stats:
  - Total Delta Ð net buying vs selling pressure for the day.
  - RF Ð Rotation Factor (explained later).
  - IBH / IBL Ð Initial Balance High and Low from the first hour of trade.
  - ACR Ð Average Candle Range, useful for gauging intraday volatility.
  - V Ð Total volume traded for the day.
- [03:45] Row-by-row statistics on the left of the TPO profile show, at each price row: This allows traders to identify high-volume, high-delta levels that may act as friction zones on retests, useful for scalp setups or anticipating pullbacks.
  - The number of TPO blocks.
  - Total volume transacted.
  - Net delta.
- [05:52] The open and close of the session are marked with an arrow and highlighted block for quick reference.
- [06:23] Heat map coloring of TPO letters: split into four zones (red ? yellow ? green ? blue), with brightness indicating time spent. This provides a visual aid to quickly identify time concentration without expanding the profile, especially helpful on neutral or overlapping days.
- [08:40] The volume profile is attached to the right of the TPO, allowing direct comparison between the time point of control and the volume point of control, with both highlighted visually.
- [09:21] Zoomed-in volume profile rows display: This makes high-activity levels and order flow imbalances instantly visible.
  - Percentage of volume at each row.
  - Color-coded positive or negative delta.
- [10:25] Practical example of fading a time/volume discrepancy: if price spends significant time at a level with little volume, while the volume POC sits higher, this can suggest bullish conditions and a potential long back toward the volume POC.
- [11:29] Preferred chart settings discussed: 30-minute time interval and 50-tick price increments, noted as adjustable based on the instrument and trader preference.
- [11:47] Functionality to merge or expand TPO blocks via double-click, useful for building composite profiles across multiple sessions.
- [12:30] Rotation Factor (RF) explained:
  - Each 30-minute period is scored +1, -1, or 0 based on directional progression.
  - The cumulative number indicates the market's attempted direction.
  - High positive values lean bullish; low negative values lean bearish.
- [13:37] Applying RF to context: If multiple days show strongly negative rotation factors but price fails to move lower (e.g. in a range), this suggests the market is trying to move down but doing a poor job, increasing the probability of an upside resolution. This addresses the key auction questions: which way is the market trying to move, and is it succeeding?
- [15:09] Time vs. Volume comparison: Combining both profiles in one view highlights discrepancies. Price spending significant time at a level without corresponding volume is often a weak move that can be considered for fading.
- [16:08] Closing summary of the template's benefits: consolidated daily stats, time vs. volume comparison, rotation factor visibility, and per-row delta and volume insights Ñ all designed to support more informed trade decisions.

We encourage reviewing these concepts carefully, practicing their application on historical sessions, and integrating them into a structured trading plan with disciplined risk management.
