# TPO Part 1

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This video kicks off a comprehensive series on TPO (Time Price Opportunity) charts, providing an essential introduction to how this powerful charting method organizes market data through time-based analysis. Viewers will learn the fundamentals of reading TPO charts, understand the critical relationship between price, time, and volume, and discover how identifying discrepancies between these three metrics can dramatically improve trading decisions and win rates.

### Detailed Timestamps:

- [00:00] Introduction to the TPO Series - Overview of TPO charts and how they differ from fixed range volume tools. Key distinction: fixed range tools measure volume, while TPO charts measure time. TPO stands for Time Price Opportunity, commonly used in stock market analysis.
- [01:30] Template Overview - Explanation of the dual-chart template using Exo Charts: TPO chart on the left and volume profile chart on the right. The combination helps identify discrepancies between time and volume.
- [02:30] Understanding TPO Letters - Breaking down the lettering system:
  - Each letter represents a 30-minute candle
  - 48 letters total per day (24 hours Ö 30 min)
  - Capital A through X covers the first 12 hours
  - Lowercase a through x covers the next 12 hours
- [04:00] Compact vs Expanded View - Why expanding TPO charts helps organize daily price action and makes it easier to track price movement throughout the day.
- [05:30] TPO is NOT a Strategy - Critical concept: TPO charts organize data but do not provide a tradable strategy on their own. Strategy comes from combining TPO with sessions and time-based analysis.
- [06:30] Limitations of Trading View Alone - Why looking only at candlestick charts without volume context makes it difficult to identify breakouts versus continued ranging.
- [07:30] Auction Market Theory Basics - The market constantly moves between balance and imbalance:
  - Balanced ranges represent fair value
  - Volume increases signal acceptance and breakouts
  - Low volume breakouts typically fail
- [09:30] The Three Key Ingredients - Price, Time, and Volume must work together. Healthy breakouts require volume confirmation; ranges show low volume with sideways price action.
- [12:30] Resistance/Support Flips - How range breakouts retest the old range high as new support, with volume confirming acceptance.
- [13:30] Comparing the Three Chart Views - Side-by-side analysis of the same price action on Trading View, Volume Profile, and TPO charts.
- [15:00] Identifying Day Types Early:
  - Balanced days - sideways, no major moves expected
  - Double distribution days - two distinct value areas
  - Imbalanced days - characterized by single prints, large directional moves
- [17:30] Live Chart Analysis Example - Walking through current day TPO with initial balance concept, showing how low-volume breakout attempts above initial balance often fail and revert back into range.
- [19:30] Time vs Volume Discrepancies - Key trading insight: when price spends significant time at a level but volume is low, it signals weakness:
  - At highs = bearish signal
  - At lows = bullish signal
- [21:00] Tick Size Settings - Explanation of TPO tick configuration:
  - Current recommendation: 50 ticks for Bitcoin's volatility
  - Lower price points (e.g., $9K BTC) used 20 ticks
  - Higher tick sizes = fewer blocks per row
  - ?? Warning: Changing ticks alters all data including value areas and POC
- [24:30] Value Area High Analysis - Why switching between time-based and volume-based value areas reveals key discrepancies. When time POC and volume POC differ significantly, it signals potential reversal opportunities.
- [26:00] Real Trade Example - $62K BTC Short - Case study showing how TPO charts revealed weak volume during the move to $62,000, supporting a high-probability short trade that played out to $50,000.
- [28:00] Summary of Key Concepts:
  - 30-minute intervals with capital/lowercase letter system
  - TPO alone is data organization, not a strategy
  - Combining price, time, and volume is essential
  - Win rates can improve dramatically when all three metrics align
