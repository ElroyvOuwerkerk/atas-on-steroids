# Types Of Traders Part 2

## Portal Summary & Highlights

We also have cheat sheets available for this topic which can be downloaded as a high-res PDF via our vault section here : https://portal.chartchampions.com/vault - download is titled ‘Types of Traders’

Welcome to the Types of Traders lesson where coach Daniel gives an overview of why he switched from scalp trading to swing trading. Stay informed, trade well!

TIMESTAMPS

00:01 – INTRO

00:36 – UTILITY

Scalp trading can require you to take +100 trades per day on low time frame charts.

Now, Daniel is more of a swing trader, taking around 1 trade per week.

You can combine the two styles, obtaining your swing trading entries with scalp trading techniques.

01:56 – IMPLEMENTATION

First, complete your HTF analysis and place alerts at areas of interest.

Once the alert is triggered, you can then zoom into the LTF and look for a ‘scalp entry’.

You can then hold that entry for a HTF idea and swing trade targets.

?? 02:52 An example of a LTF entry is shown using Orderflow from Exocharts.

You will still need to spend a considerable amount of time at your computer.

If there are high volatility days, you can always be more active in the market. You can be flexible.

Once your alert is triggered, you can zoom into LTF orderflow to make an informed decision and get the best available entry.

?? 06:14 Watch Daniel explain the steps he would go through to find a swing trade.

?? 11:14 How coach Daniel uses orderflow to obtain the best entries.

16:42 – TIPS & TRICKS

? Even if you want to be a swing trader, learning how to scalp trade can be helpful. Vice Versa.

? If you cannot take scalp trade entries, simply place a pre-set limit order at your key level (with a stop loss pre-set too).

? Don’t rush your learning. Understand that developing a trading style takes a lot of experience and TIME.

? Enjoy the process!
