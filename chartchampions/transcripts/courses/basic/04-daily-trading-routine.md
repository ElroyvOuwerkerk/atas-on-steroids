# Daily Trading Routine

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This video reveals a comprehensive daily trading routine designed to give traders a significant edge by preparing setups before they form. The strategy emphasizes planning both bullish and bearish scenarios, identifying key reference levels, analyzing context through trend direction and Elliott Wave counts, and using volume information including open interest, delta, and funding rates. By following this systematic approach, traders can eliminate emotional decision-making and execute trades with confidence and clarity.

### Detailed Timestamps:

- [00:00] Introduction to the daily trading routine philosophy: knowing the setup before it even appears, giving you an edge over competitors who are still figuring out what's happening.
- [00:34] Plan all possible trade setups - prepare both a bullish scenario and a bearish scenario at the start of each day. Sit back and let the trade come to you without feeling pressured.
- [01:11] Introduction to the daily trading sheet with key reference levels like the daily open. Understanding context behind levels (bullish daily open vs. weak open) determines how you'll trade them.
- [02:05] Context analysis - the foundation for daily structure. Three key elements:
  - Trend direction on the 15-minute/1-hour timeframe (local) and 1-day timeframe (higher term)
  - Determining if price is trending or moving sideways - this dramatically changes your trading approach
- [03:30] Higher timeframe Elliott Wave counts:
  - If in a Wave 3 trending move - be aggressive, long every dip, compound heavily, trail stops
  - If in a Wave 4 triangle - expect ranges, take tight profits, only trade near extremes
- [04:21] CVD divergences for context - showing what larger traders are doing. Example: price moving down on heavy volume but making higher lows with bullish CVD divergence indicates big traders are absorbing sell orders with limit buys.
- [05:20] Establishing your daily bias - bullish bias means looking to long dips; bearish bias means looking to short rises.
- [05:45] Important levels to mark daily:
  - CC Fibonacci - both global (rare but extremely profitable - only twice on Bitcoin in 2 years, both major tops) and local levels
  - 4-hour EMAs - the 55 and 200 EMAs as support/resistance (never traded alone, only as confluence)
  - Points of Control - daily, weekly, and monthly
- [07:42] Key horizontal levels - manually identified levels with multiple layers of confluence. These are your highest probability trade locations - identify the next major support and resistance to be patient with.
- [09:11] Volume Information (newly added section, possibly the most important):
  - Open interest from previous day
  - Daily volume and delta
  - Current funding (bullish or bearish)
- [09:46] Why recording statistics yourself is crucial - you must record data first before you can build statistical edges. Watch for confirmations like rising price + rising OI + rising volume, or divergences between these metrics.
- [10:39] Why volume information is superior to indicators - it's not a buy/sell signal. Indicators that give simple buy/sell signals lose money long-term because there's no edge when 100 people see the same thing. Hard facts allow for individual interpretation and edge.
- [12:00] The morning routine timing - wake up 45 minutes to 1 hour before London Open, spend ~25 minutes marking levels and recording statistics. Know exactly where you'll trade before the market even moves.
- [13:20] Spending 10 hours a day staring at charts won't make you profitable. Instead, pre-set your plans, take the trade when it hits your level without overthinking, then journal the results.
- [13:50] The three pillars: (1) Plan your trades, (2) Be ready to trade, (3) Understand the trade. Without a plan and understanding, you'll keep losing money and become discouraged.
- [14:34] Final takeaway: Knowing the trade before you see it makes life easier. Keep trading simple - identify setups, wait for them to come, take them, make money, sleep well at night.

Start implementing this structured routine tomorrow morning - mark your key levels, plan both scenarios, record your volume statistics, and commit to taking the trades that come to you. With consistent application, you'll transform from a reactive trader chasing setups into a prepared trader who profits with clarity and confidence.
