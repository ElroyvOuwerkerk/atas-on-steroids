TIMESTAMPS

00:01 – INTRO

00:32 – UTILITY

There are four main styles of trading:

- 1. Scalp trading
- 2. Day trading
- 3. Swing trading
- 4. Position trading (investing)

You can be all types of traders simultaneously to maximize your profits.

01:40 – IMPLEMENTATION

Scalp trading is down to very low time frames.

Day trading is typically around the 30-minute time frame up to the 4-hour time frame.

Swing traders are using the 4-hour time frame up to the Daily and Weekly.

Position traders / investors typically use the Weekly time frame or greater.

There is no right or wrong style of trading, as most styles of trading make similar profits.

If you are at the PC for long periods of time, enjoy gaming or have good knowledge of computers, then Scalp Trading is a great option.

Swing Trading is more for people with families, busy lives or overall life commitments because this is a more relaxed style of trading that requires less time at the computer.

Day Trading falls in the middle of these two, it is up to YOU to decide what is best for you.
