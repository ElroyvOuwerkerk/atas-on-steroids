# Getting Started

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This video delivers essential risk management principles for traders, focusing on two practical methods to calculate position sizes and protect capital. Learn why risking only 1% per trade is critical, how to mathematically determine your trade size based on stop loss percentages, and why developing a simple, mastered strategy beats complicated approaches every time.

### Detailed Timestamps

- [00:00] Introduction to risk management - Risk management approaches depend heavily on your account size and trading capital.
- [00:11] The reality of becoming a consistent trader - It typically takes 3-5 years to become consistently profitable. Trading is "some of the hardest easy money you can ever make" and requires significant time investment.
- [01:05] Long-term vision over short-term gains - Focus on consistency rather than chasing big single-day wins. Small gains compound over time, which is why putting all capital into one trade is dangerous.
- [01:46] The danger of high-percentage entries - Never use Binance's quick-click 25%, 50%, or 100% buttons. These features exist to take your money, not to help you trade efficiently.
- [02:30] Losing streaks are inevitable - Every trader experiences losing streaks. Risking 10%+ per trade means 10 consecutive losses can wipe out over 50% of your account.
- [03:02] Method #1: The 1% Risk Rule - Always know your entry, stop loss, and target before entering. Example: With $5,400 in your account, the maximum loss per trade is $54 (1%).
- [03:52] Position size calculation walkthrough - Using a Bitcoin example with a 4% stop loss and 10% target:
  - Identify the stop loss below a recent higher low
  - Convert stop loss percentage to decimal (4% = 0.04)
  - Formula: Risk Dollar Amount ÷ Stop Loss Decimal = Position Size
  - $54 ÷ 0.04 = $1,350 position size
  - Win scenario at 12% target = $162 profit (3:1 reward-to-risk ratio)
- [06:28] Method #2: Percentage-based risk for larger accounts - This approach works only with substantial capital. Trading with $100 makes 1% positions ($1) impractical for meaningful trading.
- [07:52] The 5% maximum trade size rule - Never risk more than 5% of total portfolio per trade. With a $5 million account, max trade is $250,000, but average trades remain at 1% (~$50,000). Following these rules ensures you'll never wipe out an account.
- [08:46] Master one area before expanding - Whether swing trading or scalping, focus on one style first. Trying to master everything results in mastering nothing.
- [09:05] Simplicity beats complexity - Information overload from hundreds of books and indicators leads to confusion. The simpler your strategy, the faster you'll recognize valid buy/sell signals.
- [09:55] The key strategy question - Before adding or removing any element, ask: "Is this increasing profitability or reducing risk?" If yes, implement it. If no, leave it out.
- [10:16] The mindset of excellence - Mediocrity doesn't win in trading or life. Approach trading with full commitment and competitive drive—half-effort produces half-results.

Take action now: Calculate your maximum 1% risk amount today, create a position-sizing formula based on your typical stop loss percentages, and commit to mastering one trading style before branching out. Implement these risk management principles consistently and you'll protect your capital while building long-term, sustainable profitability.
