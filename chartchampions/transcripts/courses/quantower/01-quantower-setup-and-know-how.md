To do this, copy the “templates” folder from the vault and paste it into the Quantower folder, where you have just installed it. Make sure you close Quantower before doing so.

Now re-open Quantower and all templates and binds will be available to load from the templates panel.

For the sessions set up and overall features, we will go over this in detail in the portion of the video to come.
