## Portal Summary & Highlights

We also have cheat sheets available for this topic which can be downloaded as a high-res PDF via our vault section here : https://portal.chartchampions.com/vault - download is titled ‘Trade Entry Checklist’

This video is part two of the trading checklist series, focusing on analyzing medium to low term timeframes for trading entries. The presenter emphasizes the importance of starting with higher term timeframe analysis to determine the overall trend and key support/resistance levels before zooming into lower timeframes to find precise entries. He also discusses the significance of understanding where price could potentially reverse, areas for quick price movement, and planning for trade management, including compounding or re-entering positions.

### Timestamps

- [00:02:48] The presenter defines low, medium, and high term timeframes, with the 1-hour and below being low, 4-hour being medium, and above 4-hour being high term.
- [00:05:07] Questions to ask when analyzing medium to low term timeframes, such as identifying the 4-hour trend, proximity to key levels, and the presence of impulsive waves from a base.
- [00:20:41] The importance of recognizing where price can move quickly, such as breaking through a triple top or moving through single prints, which lack high volume nodes.
- [00:23:32] Planning for potential re-entry if stopped out of a trade or compounding on signs of strength. The presenter emphasizes the significance of this step, especially for experienced traders.
- [00:29:19] Differentiating between being right on an overall idea and getting stopped out of a trade. The presenter provides an example of this concept using a recent trade setup.
- [00:45:50] A practical example of how the presenter combines higher timeframe analysis with lower timeframe entry triggers, such as bullish CVD divergences and trapped shorts at a key support level.
