# Severin Scalping Strategy - Execution

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session presents the third part of a structured scalping strategy focused on trade execution, covering three distinct entry types: aggressive entries, reclaim or loss entries, and backtest or retest entries. We learn how order flow analysis, delta ladder profiles, footprint statistics, and reference levels such as value area highs/lows, VWAP, and point of control are integrated to confirm entries and avoid premature trade executions. The lesson also details risk management parameters, take profit strategies based on local market structure, and includes live trade examples demonstrating the practical application of each entry type.
