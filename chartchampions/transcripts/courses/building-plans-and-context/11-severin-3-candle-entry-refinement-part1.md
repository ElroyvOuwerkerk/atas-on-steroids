# Severin's 3 Candle Entry Strategy Refinement Part 1

## Portal Summary & Highlights

In this video, we explore a powerful refinement of the three candle entry strategy that has led to an 8% increase in win rate. We'll dive deep into two high-conviction entry types—the Cherry on Top (COT) and Change of Context (COC)—that help eliminate low-conviction trades and provide clearer yes/no decisions on trade execution. These refined techniques combine specific candle formations with statistical probabilities to maximize trading success at predefined levels.

### Timestamps

- [00:00] Introduction & Overview Welcome and legal disclaimer. The lesson covers the utility, implementation, and tips for the refined three candle entry strategy.
- [00:42] Why This Refinement Matters • This builds upon the original Severin scalping strategy • Common red flags from student entries have been identified and addressed • Key benefit: An additional 8% increase in win rate through simplified decision-making
- [01:26] Three Entry Types Introduced • Cherry on Top (COT) – The highest probability entry • Change of Context (COC) – Second-best entry method • Momentum Entry – Covered in part two of the lesson
- [01:58] Core Three Candle Entry Concept Review • Candle 1: Arrival candle (approaches the level of interest) • Candle 2: Reversal candle (provides confirmation of reaction) • Candle 3: Entry candle (where the trade is executed) • Important: The second candle is always the confirmation—not based on Fibonacci retracements
- [03:59] High vs. Low Conviction Reversals • High conviction: Changes the low timeframe context • Low conviction: Typically inside candles showing indecision • Critical insight: Context is everything—without understanding context, no trade should be considered
- [05:01] Identifying Inside Candles (Low Conviction) • Inside candles trade entirely within the previous candle's range • They don't take liquidity below OR change context above • These setups should be avoided
- [06:02] Cherry on Top (COT) Entry Criteria • Must take liquidity of the previous candle (low for bullish, high for bearish) • Must close in extreme quartile (upper for bullish, lower for bearish) • Statistical backing: 88% probability of seeing follow-through with extreme quartile closes • Look for large wicks upon taking liquidity
- [07:18] COT Practical Examples • COT can combine arrival and reversal into one candle (making it a 2-candle entry) • Visual demonstration of liquidity sweep + extreme quartile close • Example shows beautiful follow-through after proper COT formation
- [09:18] Change of Context (COC) Entry Criteria • Must take out the high of previous candle for bullish reversal • Must take out the low of previous candle for bearish reversal • Uses standard 3-candle formation • Can combine with COT for even higher conviction entries
- [10:40] COC Practical Example • Demonstrates how COC changes the one-minute timeframe context • Without COC or COT, price could maintain one-timeframing in the opposite direction
- [11:37] Value of Refined Entry Strategy • Example showing a low conviction reversal that failed • Price had to "finish business" before providing a proper COC reversal • Key lesson: Wait for confirmation rather than guessing at reactions
- [12:55] Live Trade Examples • ES trade: Previous day value area high + COC entry • ES trade: Previous day VAH flip with supply confluence (17% probability increase) • Naked weekly VAH: Wait through chop for proper COC confirmation
- [14:14] Powerful Mindset Shift "I am not convincing the market to take a trade. The market has to convince me to take a trade."
- [14:58] COT + COC Combined Example • Previous week point of control level • Candle takes liquidity, closes in upper quartile, AND takes previous candle's high • This combination represents the highest conviction setup
- [15:57] Tips & Tricks Summary • Small strategy adjustments create significant performance impacts • This entry works on any timeframe due to universal statistics • Can be traded without levels, but confluence produces best results • Accept missed trades—if a low conviction candle was the reversal, move on • Strict rule-following produces the best results—don't mix entry methods
