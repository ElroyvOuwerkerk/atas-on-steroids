# Igor Live Trading 2026-11-03

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session focuses on live futures trading around the US market open, with emphasis on simple yet effective tools such as moving averages, VWAP, opening range breakouts, and key session levels. We review how to combine multiple confluences Ñ including period highs/lows, point of control, imbalance fills, and structural shifts Ñ to identify high-probability long setups on NQ and ES, while also examining the broader structure on BTC.
