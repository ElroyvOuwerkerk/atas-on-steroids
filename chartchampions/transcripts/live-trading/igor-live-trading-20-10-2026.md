# Igor Live Trading 2026-10-20

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session focuses on applying market profile and order flow concepts to NQ and ES during the New York open, with additional analysis of BTC positioning. Key educational themes include trading untested points of control, using the 15-minute opening range retest, interpreting value area shifts across A, B, C, and D sessions, and identifying absorption patterns through delta and volume clusters. We also review candle structure fundamentals, trailing stop techniques, and how to assess whether to engage or stand aside based on profile context.

### Highlights

- [00:06] Reviewing the economic calendar as part of pre-market preparation, noting upcoming inflation data and understanding how high-impact events shape intraday expectations.
- [08:19] Identifying key confluences at the open: daily untested point of control, previous day low, and an intraday gap (not a full RTH gap). We learn to distinguish between intraday gaps and true regular-trading-hours gaps when planning targets.
- [11:40] Using stacked imbalances and untested volume profiles behind price to assess downside probability. The concept of tick-1 raw data is introduced for precise level validation.
- [12:46] Example scalp short into the untested point of control:
  - Entry on the break below prior low
  - Target: daily POC to the tick
  - Exit via market order upon reaching the level
- [14:14] Pattern recognition breakdown: declining volume on retracements followed by renewed selling confirms continuation. Emphasis on recognizing similar patterns repeatedly across setups.
- [18:19] The 75% rule on 15-minute bars: expect pullback near the 75% mark of the prior bar, with the 25% level acting as support for continuation trades.
- [22:40] A-session midpoint analysis and session rotation. We learn that when the midpoint and settlement align with a low-auction area, it signals a high-probability bounce zone.
- [25:14] Lesson on emotional discipline after missing a trade: remain calm, process the setup objectively, and recognize that hindsight clarity does not mean the trade was executable in real time.
- [27:00] Watching for large sellers at resistance and anticipating full rotations before period close. Key observation: markets often clean highs/lows before expanding.
- [31:19] Explanation of A-session point of control and Initial Balance (IB) construction Ñ these levels merge visually when occurring at the same price and serve as major reference points.
- [38:19] The 15-minute backtest playbook: after the opening bar, the retest of the previous 15-minute bar's close/open area often provides the highest-probability continuation entry in trending conditions.
- [42:00] Discussion of market structure context: when value shifts higher throughout the session, bias remains with the trend; when letters remain "white" (low value) far from current price, fade risk increases.
- [44:30] Candlestick anatomy breakdown:
  - Long lower wicks with bearish close = unconfirmed reversal
  - Long upper wicks with bullish close = not fully bearish
  - Large bodies = compressed value with no rejection at extremes
- [48:00] Red flags for standing aside: price trading outside value with low-tempo rotation between value area high and low Ñ walk away and return at the next session.
- [51:19] Reading delta vs. price alignment: when delta continuously increases alongside price, avoid counter-trend fades until a clear divergence or trap pattern appears.
- [01:04:00] Risk management philosophy on scalp longs: enter with intent to trail or hold overnight, but acknowledge "it either works or it doesn't" Ñ no need for excessive long exposure if the broader thesis isn't ready.
- [01:14:00] Absorption pattern playbook explained step by step:
  - Two or more large sell orders at the same level with price refusing to drop
  - Draw a trendline across the absorption highs
  - Wait for a break and hold above the neckline
  - Look for a spiking inverted delta confirming buyers stepping in
  - Place stop below the absorption low
  - Target: at least 2:1 reward-to-risk toward the next resistance
- [01:27:00] Mental framework for taking trades: "It either works or it does not." Define risk (approximately $150/lot), define reward (300+), and remove emotional attachment from the outcome.
- [01:50:00] Trailing stop technique: rather than manually adjusting stops, place a market sell stop below swing lows (for longs) to automatically exit if structure breaks.
- [01:52:00] Two-bar candle structure method: mark the previous candle's low/high; while price remains one-time-framing in a direction, continuation bias holds. A break reverses the sequence.
- [01:58:00] End-of-session trade filtering: late-session trades require tighter criteria. Low-probability setups should be skipped even when a directional bias is clear.
- [02:00:00] Session recap emphasizing: markets will be here tomorrow Ñ no need to force trades. Patience and selective execution preserve capital and clarity.

We encourage reviewing these market profile concepts, absorption patterns, and candle structure techniques carefully, and applying them only with disciplined risk management and proper backtesting before committing capital.
