# Igor Live Trading 2026-10-09

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session reviews intraday price action across BTC, ES, and NQ futures, focusing on how to navigate a slow, range-bound market by using market profile levels, VWAP, opening range, and structure-based entries. We explore how to identify low-volatility conditions, time scalp entries using local structure and volume delta, and manage risk when target potential is limited.

- [1:00:46] Managing exits in slow conditions: Recognizing that any-other-day targets (e.g., ORB EQ at 100) may not fill on low-volatility sessions; adjusting trail logic accordingly.
- [1:02:56] Reversal vs. breakout entries: Comparing better risk-reward on reversal entries against the stability of breakout entries after consolidation energy builds.
- [1:04:30] Order flow footprint reading: Using DOM/footprint to identify large resting orders being moved around the market and interpreting intent behind spoofing or iceberg behavior.
- [1:09:40] Consistency over size: The importance of taking modest, repeatable profits (e.g., $100Ð$300) as a foundation for sustainable trading progress.
- [1:16:30] Confluence recap and failed break: Reviewing why the market lacked fuel to extend, with price returning inside the daily naked point of control.
