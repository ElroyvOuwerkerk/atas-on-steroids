# Igor Live Trading 2026-11-13

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session focuses on intraday analysis of Nvidia (NVDA), AMD, NQ, and ES futures, demonstrating how to combine volume profile, Fibonacci quartiles, opening range, VWAP, and daily point of control levels to identify high-probability trade setups. We review range analysis, acceptance inside previous day's value, orderflow confirmation through delta and absorption, and how to manage trades during trending versus ranging conditions.

Key takeaway: avoid longs at the top of an extended move; wait for pullback to the 0.25 quartile or weekly point of control around 460. - [34:15] Opening range and gap analysis: We watch for acceptance inside the previous day's range vs. a rejection at the gap fill. The V1 opening range is marked, and we track volume spikes into the open to anticipate directional bias.
- [38:00] Previous day point of control (POC) as key level: NQ trading just below previous day's POC with potential rotation down to value area low around 28214. The midpoint of chop is identified as a low-probability zone.

- [2:11:00] Opening at equilibrium challenge: Opening right at the EQ (midpoint) of a prior move removes directional confluence, making setups harder. Patience is required until price flips a key level such as the sloping trendline or angled VWAP.

We encourage reviewing these concepts carefully and applying them with disciplined risk management, always waiting for confirmation at key levels before committing to a trade.
