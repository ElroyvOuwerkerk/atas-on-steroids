# Champions 386

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session reviews current market structure across Bitcoin, the stock market, oil, gold, silver, and selected equities, with a focus on identifying critical support zones, high-confluence resistance levels, and actionable trade setups. We examine the correlation between Bitcoin and equities, discuss the importance of trade execution discipline, and explore how to combine technical analysis with long-term investment planning while managing risk through position sizing and clear invalidation levels.

- [04:01] Overview of current market context: Bitcoin, gold, oil, and stocks are all testing critical levels. Despite Friday's drop, support is holding, and Bitcoin shows relative strength compared to equities.
- [07:40] Discussion of the historical correlation between Bitcoin and the stock market: when equities drop sharply, Bitcoin typically drops as well. Divergence is rare, and the correlation remains important for directional bias.
- [09:04] Stock market analysis using value area theory:
  - Price formed a failed auction at all-time highs
  - Acceptance below value area high targets value area low
  - Target has been successfully hit, with confluence from the CC Fibonacci and prior September lows
- [11:06] Importance of distinguishing bull vs. bear market context when evaluating support. In a strong bull market, support is more likely to hold.
- [12:23] Introduction to support/resistance flip theory:
  - Lost support typically flips to resistance
  - Strength is confirmed by reclaiming and flipping back to support
  - Entry can be taken with invalidation below the fake-out low
- [14:44] Identification of the 29,000 reclaim level as the key local resistance. Failure to reclaim increases probability of a deeper move toward 26,000, which is highlighted as major technical support with multiple confluences (CC Fibonacci, prior value area high, prior all-time highs).
- [18:00] Risk management discussion: the trade-off between waiting for the perfect low versus opportunity cost of staying in cash. Dollar-cost averaging is presented as a balanced approach.
- [20:30] Pattern recognition for major lows: historical price action tends to form fake-outs at lows before V-shaped reversals. This "rhyming" behavior across multiple cycles helps identify high-probability reversal zones.
- [27:00] Importance of a long-term investment mindset for weathering drawdowns:
  - Underwater positions are opportunity if conviction remains intact
  - Long-term portfolios in strong assets historically recover
  - Side investments complement active trading income
- [30:00] Bitcoin's critical support zone discussed: value area low to uptrend anchored VWAP. Loss of this zone opens targets toward 63,000-64,000, with further downside potential to 50,000-55,000.
- [33:00] Review of a prior swing failure pattern on Bitcoin that produced a textbook long setup, demonstrating how technical price action can be read in real time rather than hindsight.
- [38:00] Mapping of upcoming long trade setups on Bitcoin:
  - Fake-out of local lows into the uptrend anchored VWAP
  - Timing matters: slower setups align better with cleaner entries
  - Invalidation levels clearly defined; prepared to take small losses
- [44:00] Identification of short trade opportunities:
  - Local resistance from the quick-move-down zone (approached cautiously)
  - Stronger confluence short at the monthly naked point of control around 80,000 with CC Fibonacci and prior high alignment
  - Entry triggered only after price returns below the high (not shorting the absolute top)
- [49:00] Trading philosophy emphasized: take the trades when they are given. Missing valid setups is more costly than taking a stopped-out trade with proper invalidation.
- [51:00] Oil analysis using pitchfork, Fibonacci, and value area confluence. Current support is weaker due to lack of confluence; loss of the uptrend anchored VWAP would be a bearish signal for a larger reversal.
- [55:00] Gold analysis: 3,900 remains a critical support level that has been identified for months. The inverse correlation with oil is noted, and a long position is being considered at this zone.
- [57:30] Google (Alphabet) investment thesis: value area low on the uptrend anchored VWAP highlighted as a key support. Discussion of limit order management and the discipline of defined invalidation at 260-250.
- [1:00:00] Amazon shown as a comparison recovery example: a 15% drop fully recovered, illustrating that strong companies tend to reclaim lost ground over time.
- [1:04:00] Reflection on limit order allocation and the lesson of balancing orders across multiple support levels to avoid being overweighted at a single zone.
- [1:08:40] Discussion of seasonal patterns in stock market lows. While March/April pivots have been common historically, waiting rigidly for seasonal lows can create missed opportunity if support hits earlier.
- [1:11:00] Explanation of funding rates as an additional profit stream when shorting:
  - Positive funding means longs pay shorts
  - Can add significant annualized yield while holding a successful short position
- [1:14:00] Portfolio allocation discussion: avoid doubling exposure to highly correlated assets (gold and silver). Half-and-half allocation is acceptable; full allocation to both is overexposure.
- [1:16:00] Historical context on the dot-com bubble and extended bear markets. Importance of being prepared to re-evaluate long-term theses if market structure fundamentally shifts.
- [1:21:00] Closing framework for trading success:
  - Execute trades when valid setups appear
  - Accept small losses as part of the process
  - Allow winners to run for home-run trades that offset multiple small losses
  - Combine technical analysis with long-term investment planning
