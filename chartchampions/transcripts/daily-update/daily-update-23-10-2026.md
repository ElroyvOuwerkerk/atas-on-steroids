## Portal Summary & Highlights

Key concepts covered include identifying unfinished highs/lows during cash session, using the Commitment of Traders report, trading with the trend rather than against it, applying fractal structure for trade confirmation, and leveraging DOM/order flow (DSF) to validate levels before entry.
