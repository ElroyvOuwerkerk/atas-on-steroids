# Daily Update BTC 29 10 2026

## Portal Summary & Highlights

This session reviews the current Bitcoin market structure around the $80,000 support zone, examining why repeated tests of this level reduce its reliability and increase the probability of a breakdown. We explore volume profile analysis, weekly naked point of control confluence, descending triangle fake-out behavior, and how to identify high-probability long and short setups at specific levels rather than reacting at exhausted support.

### Highlights

- [02:19] Volume Profile Range Selection: We review how to pull a fixed range volume profile. Two valid approaches are discussed: Both are valid, but selecting the breakdown pull isolates the key point of control relevant to current price action.
  - Pulling from the entire range including the higher consolidation zone
  - Pulling from the breakdown low to high, isolating the $80,000 consolidation volume
- [03:40] Weekly Naked Point of Control (Tether vs USD Pair): The Tether pair weekly naked point of control is treated as a stronger reference level than the USD pair. We observe strong rejection on the first test, followed by a lower high on the second test Ñ classic weakening resistance behavior.
- [06:03] Why Shorting at Tested Support Is Low Quality: Even with bearish context, shorting into the sixth or seventh test of $79,500Ð$80,000 is not advised. The correct short opportunity was yesterday at the weekly naked point of control resistance, where a clear reaction occurred. Key principle: shorts belong at resistance, not after a $2,000 drop into support.
- [07:19] Why a New Long Is Also Unattractive Here: The more frequently a level is tested, the weaker it becomes. With multiple tests already registered, taking a new long at current support offers poor risk/reward.
- [08:00] Gold Analogy Ñ Support Holding Until It Doesn't: A comparison to recent gold price action illustrates how support can technically hold while continuously printing lower highs, until the eventual breakdown. Bearish context plus repeated tests often precede breakdowns.
- [08:36] Descending Triangle Fake-Out Is More Bearish: A fake-out above the descending triangle highs, followed by a return to support, is more bearish than a standard descending triangle because:
  - Short stop-losses have been swept at the fake-out highs
  - New longs have opened into the fake-out
  - Both groups become liquidity for a subsequent breakdown
- [10:00] Monthly Point of Control Confluence: Price trading below the monthly point of control alongside the weekly naked point of control at $81,650 created strong overhead volume resistance.
- [11:18] Bearish Divergences Forming: Longs opening into lower prices are producing minor bearish divergences Ñ another signal that stacks against the long thesis at current support.
- [12:12] Previous Week Low Fake-Out Long Ñ Low Probability: A fake-out of previous week low could technically offer a long setup, but context (bearish structure, failed fake-out, exhausted support, price below anchored VWAP and CC) reduces probability significantly.
- [13:51] Single Prints as Secondary Support: The bottom of the single prints near $78,500 is identified as another potential bounce zone, but confluence remains low and context remains bearish.
- [14:22] Primary High-Probability Long Zone Ñ Weekly Value Area Low: The range point of control / monthly naked point of control / weekly value area low zone around $76,000Ð$77,000 is highlighted as the strongest confluence support. Key considerations:
  - Wait for the lowest level of the zone to be tapped Ñ do not front-run
  - Look for an intraday mini-range fake-out as the entry trigger
  - First take-profit should be a back-test to current levels, not an aggressive target
- [17:30] Second Valid Long Zone Ñ Overall Range Low: The overall range low offers a simpler, cleaner entry trigger via a standard fake-out, making it mechanically easier than the value area low trade which requires more patient intraday structure confirmation.
- [18:30] Short Trade Levels Ñ Minor Only: The next potential short setup sits at the USD pair weekly naked point of control plus CC Fibonacci Ñ classified as okay but not high-quality confluence. The high-quality short zone remains back at $88,000Ð$90,000, but probability of return there is now low.
- [20:00] Elliott Wave Context: The wave structure supports continuation lower, aligning with the broader bearish bias and reinforcing why long setups must be taken only at strong confluent support.
- [21:00] Risk Management on Position Trades: A reminder that trade and investment portfolios should be managed separately, with pre-defined invalidation levels. If the anchored VWAP and value area low support fail, the position must be closed at a loss Ñ accepting large losses occasionally is part of a system that produces large wins over time.

We encourage traders to review each highlighted level carefully, wait for proper entry triggers at high-confluence zones, and apply disciplined risk management rather than reacting at exhausted or weakening support.
